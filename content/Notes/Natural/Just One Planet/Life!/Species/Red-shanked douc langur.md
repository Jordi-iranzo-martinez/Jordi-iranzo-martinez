
The red-shanked douc langur (*Pygathrix nemaeus*) is an Old World monkey (order [[Primates]], class [[Mammalia|Mammalia]]) widely celebrated as one of the most strikingly colorful primates in the world.

Native entirely to the dense canopy forests of Indochina (primarily Laos, Vietnam, and Cambodia), this arboreal monkey is often called the "costumed ape" due to its extraordinarily vibrant, multi-colored fur.
