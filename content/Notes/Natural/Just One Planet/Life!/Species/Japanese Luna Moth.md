Japanese Luna Moth (*Actias aliena*) is a breathtakingly beautiful species of giant silk moth belonging to the family Saturniidae.

They are recognizable for their large, elegant, translucent pale green or seafoam green wings. Their wingspan typically ranges from 80 to 120 mm (3 to 4.7 inches)

[[Web/content/Notes/Natural/Just One Planet/Life!/Taxonomy/Zoology/Insects|Insects]]

Upon emerging from its silk cocoon, the adult moth possesses no mouthparts and no digestive system. It cannot eat, drink, or sip nectar.
