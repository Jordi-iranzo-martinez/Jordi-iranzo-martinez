The Giant Panda (*Ailuropoda melanoleuca*) is a world-famous bear species native to the mountain ranges of central [[China]], primarily in the Sichuan, Shaanxi, and Gansu provinces

[[Mammals]]

Bamboo makes up over 99% of a giant panda's daily diet. An adult panda must spend up to 12 to 16 hours a day eating to meet its nutritional needs.

A single panda needs to devour anywhere from 12 to 38 kilograms (26 to 84 pounds) of bamboo every single day.

To handle tough bamboo stalks efficiently, pandas evolved a unique anatomical feature: a modified wrist bone (the radial sesamoid) that acts like an opposable thumb. This "sixth digit" allows them to tightly grip, peel, and manipulate bamboo stems with incredible precision.
