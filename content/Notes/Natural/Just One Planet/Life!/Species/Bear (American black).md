The American Black Bear (*Ursus americanus*) is the smallest and most widely distributed bear species ([[Mammals]]) in [[North America]].

Up to 85% of their diet consists of plant matter, including berries, nuts, roots, grasses, and insects, supplemented occasionally by fish, carrion, and newborn deer or elk.
