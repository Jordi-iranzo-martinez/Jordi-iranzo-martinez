The resplendent quetzal (*Pharomachrus mocinno*) is a breathtakingly beautiful bird native to the cloud forests of [[Central America]], stretching from southern [[Mexico]] to western Panama.

Belonging to the trogon family (_Trogonidae_), it is globally celebrated for its vibrant iridescent plumage and holds immense cultural significance as the national bird of Guatemala.
