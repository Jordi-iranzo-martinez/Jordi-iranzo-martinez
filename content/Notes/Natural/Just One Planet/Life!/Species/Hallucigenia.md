Hallucigenia is an extinct genus of tiny, worm-like marine creatures that lived during the [[Cambrian]] Explosion, approximately 508 million years ago. Discovered in the famous Burgess Shale of [[Canada]].

It is celebrated as one of the most bizarre and structurally baffling organisms in paleontological history.

*It was named Hallucigenia because its anatomy was so surreal it resembled a dream or a hallucination*

Hallucigenia was incredibly small, averaging between 1 to 5 centimeters (under 2 inches) in length—roughly the length of a human eyebrow.
