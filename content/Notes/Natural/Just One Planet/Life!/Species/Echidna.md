Equidna

[[Mammalia|Mammalia]]


Existen cuatro especies vivas de equidna distribuidas en dos géneros principales:

- **Short-Beaked Echidna** (*Tachyglossus aculeatus*): the most common one, found all across mainland [[Australia]] and the island of Tasmania

- **Long-Beaked Echidnas** (Genus *Zaglossus*): [[New Guinea]]
