
The cuscus (genera *Phalanger* and *Spilocuscus*) is a genus of nocturnal, tree-dwelling marsupials native to the dense [[Tropical Rainforest]] of [[New Guinea]], northeastern [[Australia]], and the Maluku Islands.

Cuscuses have a highly distinct appearance, featuring a round head, a short snout, large glassy eyes adapted for night vision, and tiny ears hidden beneath dense, thick fur.

Cuscuses are strictly arboreal (living entirely in the forest canopy). They are incredibly sluggish and cautious climbers, moving slowly through the branches to avoid attracting the attention of predatory pythons and eagles.

They spend the hot tropical days curled up asleep inside hollow tree trunks or hidden under dense leaf blankets, emerging only at dusk to hunt and forage.

While their diet is heavily based on leaves, shoots, and tropical fruits, they are opportunistic omnivores that will readily consume insects, bird eggs, and small lizards if they catch them unawares.
