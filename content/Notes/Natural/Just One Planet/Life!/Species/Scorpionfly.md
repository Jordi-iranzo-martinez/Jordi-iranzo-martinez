The Scorpionflie are winged [[Web/content/Notes/Natural/Just One Planet/Life!/Taxonomy/Zoology/Insects|Insects]] (orden Mecoptera, family Panorpidae) is a completely harmless insect with an intimidating appearance, globally famous because the males possess a structures at the tip of their abdomen that closely mimics a scorpion's stinger.

The most striking feature of the scorpionfly is found exclusively on the males:

- **A Mating Tool**: The bulbous, upward-curved structure contains no venom or functional stinger. It consists of modified genital claspers used strictly to securely hold the female during copulation.

- **The Females**: Do not possess this curved appendage. Instead, their abdomen tapers into a straight, pointed ovipositor used solely for laying eggs in moist soil.
