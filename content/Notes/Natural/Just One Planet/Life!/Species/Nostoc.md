Nostoc is a genus of freshwater and terrestrial cyanobacteria that form jelly-like colonies. Although they are commonly mistaken for algae due to their blue-green color, they are actually photosynthetic bacteria capable of fixing atmospheric nitrogen.

[[Cyanobacteria]]

Under a microscope, Nostoc cells look like chains of beads or microscopic pearl necklaces. These chains are wrapped in a very dense gelatinous carbohydrate sheath that protects the cells from dehydration and ultraviolet radiation.

When conditions are wet, the colonies swell up and form spherical or irregular masses visible to the naked eye. They have a rubbery consistency and colors ranging from olive green and brown to dark blue.

They feature specialized, larger cells called heterocysts. These cells act as small anaerobic factories responsible for transforming nitrogen from the air into ammonia—an essential nutrient for plants—making Nostoc a crucial natural fertilizer for the soil.
