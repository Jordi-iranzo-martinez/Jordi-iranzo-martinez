The Japanese Giant Salamander (*Andrias japonicus*) is the second-largest amphibian in the world, surpassed only by its close relative, the South China giant salamander. 

These massive amphibians can grow up to 1.5 meters (5 feet) in length and weigh up to 25 kilograms (55 pounds). They are exceptionally long-lived, with a lifespan that can reach up to 80 years in optimal conditions.
