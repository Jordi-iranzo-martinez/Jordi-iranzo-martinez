The Vampire Squid (*Vampyroteuthis infernalis*) is a unique [[Deep Ocean]] cephalopod [[Mollusks]] that lives in the pitch-black depths of temperate and tropical oceans

It lives in the Oxygen Minimum Zone (OMZ) at depths between 600 and 900 meters (2,000 to 3,000 feet).

Because it lives in complete darkness, its body is covered in light-producing organs called photophores

Vampire squids are relatively small, reaching a maximum total length of about 30 centimeters (1 foot). 

Unlike modern squids that spawn once and die immediately after, studies indicate that female vampire squids go through multiple reproductive cycles, spawning eggs intermittently throughout their surprisingly long deep-sea lifespans.
