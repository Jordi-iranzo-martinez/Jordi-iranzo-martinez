The Green Iguana (*Iguana iguana*) also known as the American iguana, is a large, arboreal, and mostly herbivorous species of lizard

[[Reptiles]]

It is native to [[Central America]], [[South America]], and the [[Caribbean]] islands, and is one of the most recognizable reptiles in the world.

While young iguanas may occasionally consume small insects, adult green iguanas are folivores.
