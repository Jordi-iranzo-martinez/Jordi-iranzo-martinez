The discus fish (genus Symphysodon) is unanimously considered the "King of the Aquarium" due to its spectacular circular body, vibrant color palettes, and majestic, slow-moving behavior. It is a freshwater tropical fish native to the floodplains and slow-moving tributaries of the Amazon River basin in South America, belonging to the cichlid family (Cichlidae).
