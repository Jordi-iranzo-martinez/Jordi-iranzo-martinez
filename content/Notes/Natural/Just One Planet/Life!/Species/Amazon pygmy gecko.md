
Amazon pygmy gecko (*Chatogekko amazonicus*) is one of the smallest lizards in the world, growing to a maximum total length of just 24 to 30 millimeters (about 1 inch).

It is roughly the size of a small paperclip or a coin, allowing it to easily sit on the tip of a human finger.

They are native to the northern lowlands of South America. Their territory spans across the dense, closed-canopy rainforests of Brazil, Colombia, Ecuador, Peru, Venezuela, and the Guianas
