The Corsac fox (*Vulpes corsac*) is a medium-sized fox native to the steppes, semi-deserts, and deserts of Central [[Asia]].

[[Mammals]]

[[Desert]]

- Mongolia (where a large percentage of the population lives)
- Kazakhstan, Uzbekistan, and Turkmenistan
- Steppe regions of Russia (Siberia)
- Northern [[China]] (Inner Mongolia)
