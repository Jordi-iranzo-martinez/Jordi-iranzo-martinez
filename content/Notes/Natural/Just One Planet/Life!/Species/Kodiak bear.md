Kodiak bear (*Ursus arctos middendorffi*) is the largest subspecies of brown bear in the world.

Adult males commonly weigh between 360 to 635 kg (800 to 1,400 lbs), while females are smaller, typically averaging 225 to 315 kg (500 to 700 lbs). Exceptionally large males in the wild can push past 700 kg (1,500 lbs) right before winter hibernation.
