The California quail (*Callipepla californica*) is a small, ground-dwelling bird famous for its striking appearance, high adaptability, and its role as the official state bird of [[California]].

Both males and females sport a forward-curving crest or topknot made of six interlocking black feathers that resemble a dramatic comma shifting over their beak.
