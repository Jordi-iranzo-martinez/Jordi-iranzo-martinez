

Priapulids (from Priapus, Greek god of fertility), also known as "penis worms", is a phylum of unsegmented, marine burrowing worms** that have inhabited the ocean floors for over 500 million years

The front portion of their body features a highly flexible, extensible, and retractable organ called an introvert

Larger species are feared ambush predators in their muddy ecosystems

### Priapulids tafonomy

- **Priapulidae**
