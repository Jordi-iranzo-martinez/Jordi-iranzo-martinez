### Birds taxonomy

- **Palaeognathae**
	- **Struthioniformes**: ostriches (the largest and fastest running birds on Earth)
	- **Rheiformes**: rheas (large, flightless birds native exclusively to South America)
	- **Casuariiformes**: cassowaries and emus (powerful Australasian birds equipped with dangerous, dagger-like claws).
	- **Apterygiformes**: kiwis (small, nocturnal New Zealand birds that lay massive eggs relative to their body size).
	- **Tinamiformes**: tinamous - (the only paleognaths capable of flight, featuring heavily camouflaged plumage).

- **Neognathae**
	- Lineage Galloanserae (Waterfowl and landfowl)
		- **Anseriformes**: Ducks, geese, and swans
		- **Galliformes**: Chickens, turkeys, pheasants, and quails
	- Lineage Neoaves (All other modern birds)
		- Nocturnal, Aerial, and Land Birds (Strisores & Columbaves)
			- **Caprimulgiformes**: Nightjars and frogmouths
			- **Steatornithiformes**: Oilbirds
			- **Nyctibiiformes**: Potoos
			- **Podargiformes**: Frogmouths (sometimes separated from nightjars)
			- **Aegotheliformes**: Owlet-nightjars
			- **Apodiformes**: Swifts and hummingbirds
			- **Columbiformes**: Pigeons and doves
			- **Mesitornithiformes**: Mesites
			- **Pterocliformes**: Sandgrouse
		- Wading, Water, and Shorebirds (Gruiformes & Charadriiformes)
			- **Gruiformes**: Cranes, rails, and coots
			- **Podicipediformes**: Grebes
			- **Phoenicopteriformes**: Flamingos
			- **Charadriiformes**: Gulls, plovers, puffins, sandpipers, and terns
		- Core Waterbirds (Ardeae)
			- **Eurypygiformes**: Sunbitterns and kagus
			- **Phaethontiformes**: Tropicbirds
			- **Gaviiformes**: Loons / Divers
			- **Sphenisciformes**: Penguins
			- **Procellariiformes**: Albatrosses, petrels, and shearwaters
			- **Ciconiiformes**: Storks
			- **Suliformes**: Gannets, boobies, cormoranes, and frigatebirds
			- **Pelecaniformes**: Pelicans, herons, egrets, and ibises
		- Core Landbirds (Telluraves - Birds of prey and allies)
			- **Opisthocomiformes**: Hoatzins
			- **Cuculiformes**: Cuckoos and roadrunners
			- **Musophagiformes**: Turacos
			- **Otidiformes**: Bustards
			- **Cathartiformes**: New World vultures (condors and turkey vultures)
			- **Accipitriformes**: Hawks, eagles, kites, and Old World vultures
			- **Strigiformes**: Owls (night-active birds of prey)
			- **Coliiformes**: Mousebirds
			- **Leptosomiformes**: Cuckoo-rollers
			- **Trogoniformes**: Trogons and quetzals
			- **Bucerotiformes**: Hornbills and hoopoes
			- **Coraciiformes**: Kingfishers, bee-eaters, and rollers
			- **Piciformes**: Woodpeckers, toucans, and barbets
			- **Cariamiformes**: Seriemas
			- **Falconiformes**: Falcons, kestrels, and caracaras
			- **Passeriformes**: Songbirds and Perching birds
