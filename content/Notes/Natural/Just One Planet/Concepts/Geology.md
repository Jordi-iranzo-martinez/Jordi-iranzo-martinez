
Geology is the scientific study of the Earth, including its composition, structure, physical properties, history, and the dynamic processes that shape it over time. It provides the literal foundation for our planet's landscapes and ecosystems, helping us understand how our physical world evolved over billions of years.




The [International Geoscience and Geoparks Programme](https://www.unesco.org/en/iggp) (IGCP), by UNESCO, and the [UNESCO Global Geoparks](https://www.unesco.org/en/iggp/geoparks/about) as laboratories for sustainable development that promote geoheritage and the sustainability of local communities
