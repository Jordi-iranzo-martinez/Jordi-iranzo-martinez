
Coronavirus disease 2019 (COVID-19) is a highly contagious respiratory infection caused by the SARS-CoV-2 virus. First identified in late 2019, the disease led to a major global pandemic. While the World Health Organization (WHO) declared an end to the global emergency phase in 2023, the virus remains an active public health focus. It continues to mutate into new strains (such as the recent JN.1 sub-lineages and XFG variants) that trigger localized seasonal surges. 

Symptoms typically appear 2 to 14 days after exposure. The virus predominantly targets the respiratory system but can affect multiple organs:

- **Most Common:** Fever, chills, a dry cough, sore throat, nasal congestion, and generalized fatigue.

- **Other Symptoms:** Loss of taste or smell, muscle aches, headaches, and gastrointestinal issues like diarrhea or nausea.

- **Warning Signs for Emergency Care:** Difficulty breathing, persistent chest pain or pressure, new confusion, or pale, gray, or blue skin and lips. _Seek immediate medical help if these occur._
