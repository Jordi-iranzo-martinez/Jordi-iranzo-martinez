Stress is a natural physiological and psychological response developed by human beings to adapt to and survive perceived threats, demands, or sudden changes in their environment. While acute stress can temporarily boost focus and energy, chronic stress is an invisible hazard that fundamentally threatens long-term health and subjective happiness.
