Te [Convention on the Conservation of Migratory Species of Wild Animals](https://www.cms.int/) (Bonn Convention)

- Appendix I. strict protections: Species threatened with extinction are listed on Appendix I, legally obligating member states to strictly prohibit the taking or harvesting of these animals and protect their critical habitats.

- Appendix II. International agreements: Migratory species that would significantly benefit from international cooperation are listed on Appendix II, encouraging nations to conclude regional binding agreements or memorandums of understanding.


### Regional agreement

- **Agreement on the Conservation of Albatrosses and Petrels** (ACAP): [[Birds]]

- **African-Eurasian Migratory Waterbirds Agreement** (AEWA): [[Birds]] related to [[Louisiana Bayous]]

- **Agreement on the Conservation of Small Cetaceans of the Baltic, North East Atlantic, Irish and North Seas** (ASCOBANS): [[Cetaceans]]

- **Agreement on the Conservation of Cetaceans of the Black Sea, Mediterranean Sea and Contiguous Atlantic Area** (ACCOBAMS): [[Cetaceans]]

- **Agreement on the Conservation of Populations of European Bats** (EUROBATS): [[Chiroptera]]
