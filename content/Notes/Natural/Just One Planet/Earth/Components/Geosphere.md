
Geosphere refers to the solid, non-living parts of the Earth, comprising all the rock, soil, minerals, and landforms from the planet's outer crust down to its deepest interior core.

- **Crust**: The outermost, thinnest layer where we live. It includes the continents (continental crust, made mostly of granite) and the ocean floors (oceanic crust, made mostly of dense basalt).

- **Mantle**: The thickest layer of the Earth, making up about 84% of the planet's volume. It is composed of hot, semi-solid silicate rock. The upper part of the mantle, combined with the crust, forms the rigid lithosphere, which is broken into tectonic plates that slowly glide over a more fluid layer called the asthenosphere.

- **Core**: The dense, metallic center of the Earth made primarily of iron and nickel. It is split into two parts:
	- **Outer core** (whose movement generates the Earth's magnetic field)
	- **Inner core**: solid, intensely hot under immense pressure.
