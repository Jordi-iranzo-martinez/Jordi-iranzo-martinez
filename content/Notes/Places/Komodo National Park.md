Komodo National Park is an extraordinary Indonesian archipelago located between the islands of Sumbawa and Flores. Established in 1980, the park spans over 1,700 square kilometers of volcanic islands, rugged savannahs, and some of the most biodiverse marine habitats on Earth. It is famously the only place in the world where you can see Komodo dragons in their native wild habitat.



It was designated a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 1986
