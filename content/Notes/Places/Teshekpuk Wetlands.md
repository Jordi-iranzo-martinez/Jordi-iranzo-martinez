
The Teshekpuk Wetlands (concentrated around Teshekpuk Lake) are located on the North Slope of Alaska, United States. Spanning roughly 1.7 million acres within the National Petroleum Reserve-Alaska (NPR-A), this mosaic of tundra, shallow lakes, and rivers is widely considered by ecologists to be one of the most important and ecologically significant wetland complexes in the entire circumpolar Arctic.
