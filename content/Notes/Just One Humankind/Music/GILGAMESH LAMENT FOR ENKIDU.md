


And Gilgamesh wept bitter tears, and he said: ‘He who was my companion, through hardship, and adventure, is now gone forever

He was the axe at my side in which my arm trusted.
He was the sword in my belt, and the shield in front of me.
He was my festive robe, and the whole of my delight.
An evil wind has risen up and stolen him from me!
