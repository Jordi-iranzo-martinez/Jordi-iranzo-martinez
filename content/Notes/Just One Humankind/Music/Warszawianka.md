This version is a socialist and labor movement anthem written between 1879 and 1883 by the poet Wacław Święcicki while imprisoned in Warsaw.

It became the definitive anthem during the 1905 Russian Revolution, specifically during the bloody worker uprisings in Warsaw against Tsarist rule.

This is the version that spread globally. 

Vladimir Lenin adopted it for the Russian Bolsheviks (known as _Varshavyanka_), and it was adapted in Spain in 1933 to become the famous CNT/FAI anarchist anthem "A las barricadas".
