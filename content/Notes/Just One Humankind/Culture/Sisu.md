Sisu is a unique Finnish cultural concept and word that has no direct English translation. It describes a form of extraordinary courage, stoic resilience, tenacity, and unrelenting grit in the face of extreme adversity.

While words like "resilience" mean bouncing back after a setback, sisu is the inner strength that pushes you to keep fighting when you have completely exhausted all your physical and mental strength. It is about taking action when the situation seems entirely hopeless.

The core pillars that define _sisu_ include:

- **Action Against the Odds:** It is not just a philosophy or a feeling; 

It is the physical act of moving forward even when the chances of success are close to zero.

It is practiced with humility. It is a quiet, internet strength, not done for show or alien praise.

It is a cornerstone of the Finnish character. Historically, it defined how the nation survived brutal winter climates and complex geopolitical conflicts, most famously during the Winter War against the Soviet Union.
