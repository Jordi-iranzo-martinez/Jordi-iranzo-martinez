Jack Dempsey ("Manassa Mauler") (left) held the World Heavyweight Championship from 1919 to 1926. Benny Leonard (right) was regarded as one of the greatest World Lightweight Champions of all time. And Harry Houdini (center) was the world-famous escape artist and magician

The image was captured by the Bain News Service agency during a promotional publicity event designed to entertain American troops and soldiers (who can be seen smiling in the background).

At that time, both Jack Dempsey and Benny Leonard were at the absolute peak of their careers as world boxing champions.

The "stomach punch challenge" was one of Harry Houdini's most famous onstage demonstrations. He frequently invited the strongest men in his audience to come up on stage and punch him in the abdomen as hard as they could.
