"The Death of Neda" refers to the tragic, fatal shooting of Neda Agha-Soltan on June 20, 2009, in Tehran, Iran.

Her death, captured on a bystander's mobile phone camera and immediately uploaded to the internet, became the definitive global symbol of resistance against the Iranian regime during the 2009 Green Movement uprisings.

Neda Agha-Soltan, a 26-year-old philosophy student and travel agency worker, was stuck in traffic with her music teacher near the anti-regime protests. The demonstrations broke out after millions of citizens disputed the highly controversial, allegedly rigged re-election victory of hardline President Mahmoud Ahmadinejad.
