Clíodhna (pronounced KLEE-nah, sometimes anglicized as Cleena) is an Irish goddess of love, beauty, and the sea, as well as the Queen of the [[Banshee]]s and the fairy folk (síoga) of South Munster.

A member of the Tuatha Dé Danann (the supernatural race of deities in Irish myth), she is often described as a daughter of Gebann (chief druid to the sea god Manannán mac Lir) or of Manannán himself. She is closely associated with County Cork, with her fairy palace hidden inside the rocky hill of Carrigcleena.

Clíodhna is famous for keeping three brightly colored, otherworldly birds that feed on apples from a magical tree. Their sweet song lulls the sick and wounded into a deep sleep, healing them completely when they awake.

In legend, she left the Otherworld (*Tír Tairngire*) out of love for a mortal warrior named Ciabhán. While she was waiting on the shore near Glandore Harbour, a massive, sudden wave (sent by Manannán mac Lir) swept her back into the sea. Today, the roaring tides at Glandore are still called *Tonn Chlíodhna*, and every ninth wave is said to belong to her.
