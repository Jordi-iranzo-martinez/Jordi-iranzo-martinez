Susanoo-no-Mikoto is the tempestuous Japanese Shinto god of storms, the sea, and battles, famous for his wild, unpredictable temper and his legendary slaying of the eight-headed serpent Yamata-no-Orochi.

*[[Ancient Japan]]*

He was born from the nose of the creator god [[Izanagi]] as he washed his face after escaping the underworld—making Susanoo the younger brother of [[Amaterasu]] (the sun goddess, born from Izanagi's left eye) and [[Tsukuyomi]] (the moon god, born from his right eye).

Fierce, chaotic, and destructive, Susanoo wreaked havoc in the heavenly realm (*Takamagahara*), destroying his sister Amaterasu's rice fields and driving her to hide in a cave (plunging the world into darkness). As punishment, the gods stripped him of his beard and fingernails and banished him to Earth.

Redeeming himself on Earth in Izumo Province, Susanoo rescued a maiden named Kushinadahime from Yamata-no-Orochi, a monstrous eight-headed, eight-tailed dragon-serpent.

He tricked the beast into drinking eight vats of strong sake until it fell asleep, then chopped it to pieces—discovering the legendary sword Kusanagi-no-Tsurugi (*Grass-Cutting Sword*) inside one of its tails, which he gifted to Amaterasu to make amends.
