Omoikane (思兼 or 思金, also called Tokoyo-no-Omoikane) is the Japanese Shinto god of wisdom, intelligence, and counsel, revered as the chief strategist and problem-solver of the heavenly gods (kami).

*[[Ancient Japan]]*

His name translates roughly to _"combining thoughts"_ or _"holding the wisdom of many minds in one"_—reflecting his ability to think with the collective intellect of thousands of individuals at once.

He is the son of [[Takamimusubi-no-Kami]], one of the primordial creator deities in Shinto mythology.

Omoikane is best known for devising the brilliant plan to bring back the sun goddess [[Amaterasu]] after her brother Susanoo's rampage drove her to hide inside the heavenly rock cave (_Ama-no-Iwato_), plunging the world into darkness. Omoikane orchestrated a joyful festival outside the cave featuring sacred mirrors, jewels, crowing roosters, and Ame-no-Uzume's comedic dance—sparking Amaterasu's curiosity until she peeked out and light returned to the world.

Today, Omoikane is popularly worshipped by students, scholars, and examinees praying for academic success, clarity of thought, and good judgment.
