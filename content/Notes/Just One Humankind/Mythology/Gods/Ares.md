Ares is the Greek god of war, representing the raw, untamed, and brutal side of battle and bloodshed (as opposed to his sister Athena, who represented military strategy and wisdom).

*[[Ancient Greeks]]*

One of the Twelve Olympians, Ares is the son of [[Zeus]] (king of the gods) and [[Hera]] (queen of the gods). His Roman counterpart is Mars.

He is typically depicted wearing a helmet and carrying a spear and shield. His sacred animals include the vulture, dog, wild boar, and serpent, and he was often accompanied into battle by his sons Phobos (Fear) and Deimos (Terror).

Despite his ferocity, Ares was frequently humbled in myth—such as being wounded by the mortal hero [[Diomedes]] (with Athena's help) during the Trojan War, or being trapped in an invisible net by Hephaestus during his affair with [[Aphrodite]].
