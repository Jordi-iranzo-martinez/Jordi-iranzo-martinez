A Nekomata (猫又, literally "forked cat") is a powerful two-tailed cat yōkai from Japanese folklore, closely related to—and often considered an older, more dangerous evolution of—the Bakeneko.

Japanese legends describe two distinct types of Nekomata:

- **Domestic Nekomata**: Ordinary house cats that live for decades, grow unusually large, and see their single tail split down the middle into two.

- **Mountain Nekomata**: Wild, ferocious feline beasts living deep in the mountains that hunt and devour travelers.

Beyond walking on their hind legs and speaking human speech, Nekomata are notorious for raising and controlling corpses like puppets by waving their twin tails, as well as conjuring ghostly fireballs (_hitodama_) that can spark mysterious house fires.

Domestic Nekomata are especially feared for holding grudges against cruel or neglectful owners, haunting their households with poltergeist-like disturbances or shapeshifting into human form to deceive them.
