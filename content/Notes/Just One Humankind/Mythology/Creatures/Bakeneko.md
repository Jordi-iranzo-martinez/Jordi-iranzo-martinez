A Bakeneko (化け猫, literally "changed cat" or "monster cat") is a supernatural feline spirit (yōkai) from Japanese folklore that gains magical, shape-shifting powers after reaching a very old age or growing to an unusual size

*[[Ancient Japan]]*

According to Edo-period legends, an ordinary house cat can turn into a bakeneko if it lives for many years (often 13+ years), reaches a certain weight (around 3.75 kg / 8 lbs), or is allowed to keep a long tail—which is one historical reason Japanese Bobtail cats became popular.

Once transformed, a bakeneko can walk on its hind legs, speak human languages, conjure ghostly fireballs (_hitodama_), raise the dead like puppets, and shape-shift into human form (often disguising itself as its own master after devouring them).

They are closely related to the nekomata, an even older and more powerful cat yōkai whose tail splits into two.
