A Futakuchi-onna (二口女, literally "two-mouthed woman") is a creepy yōkai from Japanese folklore who appears to be an ordinary woman from the front, but hides a second, ravenous mouth on the back of her head beneath her hair

*[[Ancient Japan]]*

Beneath her long dark hair, her skull splits open to reveal fully formed lips, sharp teeth, and a tongue.

The second mouth has a mind of its own and controls her hair like long, sinuous tentacles. When no one is looking, the hair reaches out to grab massive amounts of food—often rice balls or noodles—and shovels it directly into the mouth at the back of her head.

If the second mouth isn't fed enough, it shrieks and screeches loudly, causing the woman excruciating pain until she feeds it.

In most legends, a woman becomes a Futakuchi-onna due to a curse—either because a stingy miser marries a woman who seemingly eats nothing (only to discover her secret mouth devouring his entire pantry), or as punishment for a wicked stepmother who starved her stepchild to death while feeding her own child well.
