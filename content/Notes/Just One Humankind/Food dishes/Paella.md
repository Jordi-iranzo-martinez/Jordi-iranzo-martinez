
Paella is an iconic Spanish rice dish originating from the Albufera lagoon area of [[Valencia]], cooked in a wide, shallow two-handled steel pan (also called a paella or paellera).

*[[Spain]]*

Rather than a single fixed recipe, it is a cooking technique centered on short-grain rice absorbing a rich, saffron-infused broth in a thin layer without being stirred.

Main Traditional Varieties

- **Paella Valenciana** (The Original). The authentic regional classic—protected as a Cultural Heritage Asset in the Valencian Community—is made with rabbit, chicken, _ferraúra_ (flat green beans), _garrofó_ (large white butter beans), grated tomato, olive oil, water, saffron, rosemary, and sweet paprika (sometimes with _vaquetes_ snails or artichokes in season). It never includes seafood or chorizo.

- **Paella de Marisco** (Seafood Paella). A coastal favorite cooked with fish stock (_fumet_) and topped with prawns, langoustines, mussels, clams, and squid or cuttlefish.

- **Paella Mixta** (Mixed Paella). Popular outside Valencia and in tourist restaurants, combining meat (usually chicken) and seafood in the same pan—often frowned upon by Valencian purists.

- **Paella de Verduras** (Vegetable Paella). A meat-free version loaded with seasonal vegetables such as artichokes, asparagus, bell peppers, green beans, and mushrooms.

Key Elements of a Great Paella

- **The Rice**. Short-grain, high-absorption varieties grown in Valencia—such as Bomba, Senia, or Bahía—are essential because they soak up intense flavor without turning mushy.

- **The *Socarrat***. The prized, slightly caramelized, crispy layer of toasted rice that forms on the very bottom of the pan during the final minutes of cooking over high heat.

- **Wood-Fire Tradition**. Traditionally cooked outdoors over burning orange-tree wood (leña de naranjo), which imparts a subtle smoky aroma and gives precise heat control.

- **Eaten at Lunch**. In Spain, paella is strictly a midday meal (often a Sunday family ritual) rather than a dinner dish, as the heavy rice takes time to digest.
