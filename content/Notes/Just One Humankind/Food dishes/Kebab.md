
Kebab is a broad family of cooked meat dishes originating in the Middle East and Central Asia—most famously Turkey, Iran, and the Levant—where marinated meat is grilled on skewers or roasted on a vertical rotisserie.*

*The word comes from the Persian kabāb (and Arabic kabāb), originally meaning "fried" or "roasted" meat over open fire.*

Medieval Turkic and Persian soldiers and nomads skewered chunks of lamb or mutton on their swords to roast over campfires—a practical technique refined in the royal kitchens of the Ottoman and Safavid empires.

Most Famous Types of Kebab

- **_Döner Kebab_ (Turkey).** Seasoned layers of lamb, beef, or chicken stacked on a tall vertical spit that rotates slowly beside a heat source. Shaved into thin slices, it is served on a plate with rice and salad or wrapped in flatbread (_dürüm_) or pita—and inspired Greek _gyros_ and Levantine _shawarma_.

- **_Shish Kebab_ (Turkey & Middle East).** Cubes of marinated lamb, beef, or chicken threaded onto metal or wooden skewers (_şiş_ means "skewer" in Turkish) alongside bell peppers, onions, and tomatoes, then grilled over charcoal.

- **_Adana & Urfa Kebab_ (Turkey).** Hand-minced lamb kneaded with tail fat and spices, pressed onto wide flat iron skewers, and grilled over hot coals. _Adana kebab_ is fiery with red chili flakes, while _Urfa kebab_ is milder and smoky.

- **_Chelow Kabab_ (Iran).** Iran's national dish, pairing saffron-steamed basmati rice (_chelow_) with grilled skewers such as _kabab koobideh_ (seasoned ground lamb or beef) or _jujeh kabab_ (saffron-and-lemon marinated chicken), served with grilled tomatoes, sumac, and raw onion.

- **_Shawarma_ (The Levant).** Thinly sliced marinated lamb, chicken, or beef roasted on a vertical spit, typically wrapped in flatbread (_khubz_ or _lavash_) with garlic sauce (_toum_), tahini, pickles, and fries.

- **_Seekh & Galouti Kebab**_ (South Asia). Introduced to India and Pakistan via Mughal cuisine; _seekh kebab_ features spiced minced meat molded onto skewers and cooked in a clay _tandoor_ oven, while _galouti kebab_ from Lucknow is famed for its melt-in-the-mouth, finely ground spiced lamb patties.

Classic Accompaniments

- **Flatbreads and rice**. Warm _lavash_, _pita_, _naan_, or saffron rice.
- **Sauces and garnishes**. Yogurt-garlic sauce, tahini, spicy chili paste (_ezme_ or _harissa_), sumac-dusted red onions, grilled tomatoes, and pickled vegetables.
