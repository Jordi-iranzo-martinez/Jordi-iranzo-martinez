Spartacus was a Thracian slave who is sold to Lentulus Batiatus to be trained as a gladiator in Capua. After rebelling against the cruelty of his masters, Spartacus leads a massive slave escape that quickly escalates into a full-scale revolution against the Roman Republic (known historically as the Third Servile War).

The Roman Senate, terrified by the growing strength of the slave army, sends its best legions under the command of the ambitious patrician Marcus Licinius Crassus to crush the rebellion once and for all.
