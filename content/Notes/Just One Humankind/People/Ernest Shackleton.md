
Sir Ernest Henry Shackleton (February 15, 1874 – January 5, 1922) was an Anglo-Irish Antarctic explorer celebrated as one of the principal figures of the Heroic Age of [[Antarctic]] Exploration. 

Although he never reached the geographic South Pole himself, he achieved global renown for his extraordinary leadership, resilience, and devotion to keeping his crew alive under catastrophic conditions—most famously during the Imperial Trans-Antarctic Expedition (1914–1917) aboard the ship Endurance.
