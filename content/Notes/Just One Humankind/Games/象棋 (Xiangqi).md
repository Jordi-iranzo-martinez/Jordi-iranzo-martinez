Xiangqi (象棋), known in English as Chinese Chess, is a two-player abstract strategy board game representing a battle between two ancient Chinese armies (traditionally Red vs. Black or Blue).

Like Western chess, it evolved from the ancient Indian game _chaturanga_, but it uses a unique board and fast-paced, attacking rules.

Unlike Western chess, the flat disk-shaped pieces sit and move on the intersections of the lines rather than inside the squares. The board has 9 vertical files and 10 horizontal ranks.

- **The River** (楚河 漢界) divides the board horizontally in half, affecting movement (Elephants cannot cross it, while Soldiers gain sideways movement once across).

- **The Palaces**: Each side has a 3×3 grid marked with diagonal lines at its base; the General and Advisors cannot leave this zone.


The 7 Piece Types (16 Pieces per Side)

| **General** _(King)_    | 帥 / 將 | 1   | Moves 1 point orthogonally inside the Palace. _(Flying General rule: the two Generals cannot face each other on an open file without a piece in between)._ |
| ----------------------- | ----- | --- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Advisor / Guard**     | 仕 / 士 | 2   | Moves 1 point diagonally inside the Palace.                                                                                                                |
| **Elephant / Minister** | 相 / 象 | 2   | Moves exactly 2 points diagonally ("field" shape). Cannot jump over pieces or cross the river.                                                             |
| **Horse**               | 傌 / 馬 | 2   | Moves 1 point orthogonally followed by 1 point diagonally (like a Knight, **but can be blocked** if a piece occupies the adjacent orthogonal point).       |
| **Chariot** _(Rook)_    | 俥 / 車 | 2   | Moves any distance orthogonally (horizontally or vertically). It is the strongest piece.                                                                   |
| **Cannon**              | 炮 / 砲 | 2   | Moves like a Chariot, **but to capture it must jump over exactly one intervening piece** (friendly or enemy).                                              |
| **Soldier** _(Pawn)_    | 兵 / 卒 | 5   | Moves 1 point forward. After crossing the river, it can also move 1 point sideways (never backward).                                                       |

The goal is to checkmate or stalemate the opponent's General (unlike Western chess, leaving your opponent with no legal moves is a win, not a draw).
