
The [Serengeti National Park](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Serengeti+National+Park/@-2.4221555,34.6541987,8900m/data=!3m1!1e3!4m6!3m5!1s0x1832cdd680dac6d1:0x2ea157f39deb3945!8m2!3d-2.4162136!4d34.6856507!16zL20vMGdrM24?entry=ttu&g_ep=EgoyMDI2MTEwMi4wIKXMDSoASAFQAw%3D%3D) (Tanzania, [[Web/content/Notes/Just One Planet/Wonderful Places/Africa|Africa]]) is one of the most famous wildlife sanctuaries in the world.

Spanning approximately 14,750 square kilometers (5,700 square miles) of savanna, grassland plains, woodlands, and riverine environments

It is globally celebrated for hosting the most complex and intact wildlife ecosystem on Earth.

The Serengeti is the main stage for the Great Migration, the largest coordinated overland movement of wildlife on the planet.
