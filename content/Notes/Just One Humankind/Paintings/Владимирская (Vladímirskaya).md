Vladimirskaya (Владимирская) refers to the Theotokos of Vladimir—one of the most revered icons in Orthodox Christianity—

Known in Russian as the _Vladimirskaya Bogomater_, this 12th-century Byzantine icon of the Virgin Mary and the Christ Child is considered one of the greatest masterpieces of medieval art and a historic protector of Russia.

Painted in Constantinople around 1131, it was sent as a gift to Grand Duke Yuri Dolgoruky of Kyiv. In 1155, his son Andrey Bogolyubsky moved it to the city of Vladimir, giving the icon its name, before it was eventually transferred to Moscow in 1395.

It belongs to the _Eleusa_ ("Tenderness") iconographic tradition, depicting the Christ Child nestling cheek-to-cheek against his mother with one arm around her neck.

Today, the original icon is housed in the Church of St. Nicholas in Tolmachi, which serves as both an active church and part of the State Tretyakov Gallery in Moscow.
