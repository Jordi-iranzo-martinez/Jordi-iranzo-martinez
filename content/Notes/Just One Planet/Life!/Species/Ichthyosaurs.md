Ichthyosaurs (meaning "fish lizards") were a highly successful order of large marine reptiles that dominated Earth's oceans during the [[Mesozoic]] Era.

They first appeared in the Early [[Triassic]] period, thrived spectacularly throughout the Jurassic, and ultimately went extinct during the Late [[Cretaceous]], roughly 250 to 90 million years ago.

Because they occupied the exact same ecological niche that modern dolphins and killer whales fill today, they independently evolved an almost identical body plan search:

- **Hydrodynamic Design**: They possessed streamlined, teardrop-shaped bodies, a large dorsal fin to stabilize themselves, and a powerful, vertically oriented crescent tail fin used to propel themselves through open ocean water.

- **Reptilian Breathers**: Just like modern whales and dolphins, ichthyosaurs **lacked gills and had to constantly surface to breathe atmospheric air** through nostrils located high up on their snouts.

- **Limbs to Flippers**: Their land-dwelling legs completely transformed over millions of years into rigid, bone-packed paddle flippers used strictly for steering.
