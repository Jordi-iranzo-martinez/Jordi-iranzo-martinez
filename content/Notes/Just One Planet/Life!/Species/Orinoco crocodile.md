
The Orinoco crocodile (*Crocodylus intermediate*) is a critically endangered apex predator entirely endemic to the Orinoco River basin in northern [[South America]], primarily within [[Venezuela]] and [[Colombia]].

It holds the distinction of being the largest species of crocodilian in the Americas, yet it is also one of the most fragile and threatened reptiles on the planet.

The Orinoco crocodile is a master aquatic hunter positioned at the absolute top of the wetland food chain:

Because of their elongated snouts, their diet is heavily piscivorous (fish-based). They feed extensively on large river fish like catfish and piranhas.

As they grow larger, they readily shift to hunting land mammals that approach the muddy banks to drink. Their prey includes capybaras, caimans, turtles, monkeys, and even cattle or horses swept into the river currents.
