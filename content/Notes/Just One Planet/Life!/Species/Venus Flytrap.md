The Venus Flytrap (*Dionaea muscipula*) is a endemic [[Plants]] native of a tiny coastal region in the the states of North Carolina and South Carolina ([[United States of America]]).

The vast majority of wild populations live within a mere 100-kilometer (60-mile) radius around the city of Wilmington, North Carolina

They live in coastal, boggy savannas dominated by longleaf pine trees.

They thrive in wet, acidic soils composed mostly of peat moss and sand. These soils are completely flooded for much of the year and lack essential nutrients like nitrogen and phosphorus. This is exactly why the plant evolved to hunt insects: it relies on its prey to get the nutrients the soil cannot provide. ([[Web/content/Notes/Just One Planet/Life!/Ecosytems/Soil|Soil]])
