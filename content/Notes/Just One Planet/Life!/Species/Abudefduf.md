Abudefduf is a genus of tropical and subtropical marine fish in the damselfish family (*Pomacentridae*), commonly known as sergeant majors or sergeants.

*The name Abudefduf derives from the Arabic words abu ("father" or "the one with") and def ("side"), combined with the intensive plural suffix -duf, translating roughly to "the one with prominent sides". Their common name, "sergeant major," refers to their bold vertical dark bars, which resemble military rank insignia, as well as their assertive behavior on the reef.*

Comprising around 20 to 21 recognized species, they are among the largest and most abundant damselfishes found on shallow coral and rocky reefs worldwide.

They have deep, oval, laterally compressed bodies with forked tails and typically grow between 13 and 30 centimeters (5–12 in) long. Most species display silver, white, bluish, or yellow-tinted bodies marked with five to seven dark vertical bands.

Notable Species:

- **Atlantic Sergeant Major** (*Abudefduf saxatilis*). Widely distributed across the tropical Atlantic Ocean and Caribbean Sea, recognized by five black vertical bars and a bright yellow upper back.

- **Indo-Pacific Sergeant** (*Abudefduf vaigiensis*). Very similar in appearance to _A. saxatilis_, inhabiting coral reefs and lagoons across the Indo-Pacific and Red Sea.

- **Scissortail Sergeant** (*Abudefduf sexfasciatus*). Native to the Indo-Pacific, easily distinguished by the dark black stripes extending along the upper and lower lobes of its forked tail.

- **Panamic Sergeant Major** (*Abudefduf troschelii*). Common along rocky reefs in the eastern Pacific from California to Peru.

Unlike many solitary, strictly territorial damselfishes, _Abudefduf_ species often form large, active schools above reefs to feed on zooplankton, algae, small invertebrates, and the eggs of other fish.

During breeding, males turn a darker bluish-purple hue, prepare a nest patch on rock or coral, and fiercely guard the fertilized eggs against predators—even charging at much larger fish and snorkelers.
