The Hercules Beetle (*Dynastes hercules*) is one of the largest flying insects in the world and a member of the rhinoceros beetle family (Scarabaeidae) ([[Web/content/Notes/Just One Planet/Life!/Taxonomy/Zoology/Insects|Insects]]).

It is native to the tropical rainforests of Central and South America, as well as parts of the Lesser Antilles.

Males can reach lengths of up to 17.5 centimeters (7 inches) including their horns. Their upper horn grows directly forward from the thorax, while a smaller lower horn emerges from the head, creating a powerful pincer.

Females completely lack horns, have a more rounded shape, and are significantly smaller, rarely exceeding 8 centimeters in length.

The massive horns of the male are not used for gathering food or attacking other animals. They are specialized tools evolved strictly for combat against rival males.
