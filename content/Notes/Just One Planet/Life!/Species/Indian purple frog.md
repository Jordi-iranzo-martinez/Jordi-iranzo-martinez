The Indian purple frog (*Nasikabatrachus sahyadrensis*)

[[Amphibians]]

This species is strictly endemic to the Western Ghats mountain range in southern [[India]], meaning it is found nowhere else in the wild.


When the heavy monsoon rains arrive (usually around May), the frogs emerge to the surface, strictly for reproduction.

The males climb out near torrential mountain streams and emit a distinct, clucking call from the mouths of their burrows to attract females.
