The Golden Eagle (*Aquila chrysaetos*) is one of the largest, fastest, and most widely distributed birds of prey on the planet.

It belongs to the family _Accipitridae_ and inhabits a vast range across the Northern Hemisphere, spanning [[North America]], [[Europe]], [[Asia]], and northern [[Web/content/Notes/Just One Planet/Audiovisual/Documentaries/Africa]].
