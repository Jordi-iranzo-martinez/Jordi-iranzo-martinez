The Eurasian wolf (*Canis lupus lupus*)

[[Europe]]

The Eurasian wolf (Canis lupus lupus), also known as the common wolf, is the largest and most widely distributed subspecies of gray wolf in the Old World. Once widespread across nearly the entire Eurasian continent, it is currently experiencing a significant and highly debated ecological comeback in Western Europe.
