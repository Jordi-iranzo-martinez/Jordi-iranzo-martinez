Groenlandaspis sp. is a placoderm of the Late [[Devonian]] period (approximately 383 to 360 million years ago).

Its most defining feature was its heavy armor plating, particularly a distinct thoracic (chest) shield that rose into a low, pyramid-like peak along its back

Its fossilized armor plates have been unearthed in:

- [[Greenland]]
- [[North America]] (Pennsylvania)
- [[Europe]]
- [[Australia]]
- [[Antarctica]] (which had a warm, subtropical climate at the time)
