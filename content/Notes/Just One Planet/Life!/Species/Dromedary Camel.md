The Dromedary Camel (*Camelus dromedarius*)

[[Mammals]]

[[Desert]]

The dromedary has one prominent hump on its back.

The dromedary camel was first domesticated around 3000 to 2500 BCE in the Southeastern Arabian Peninsula (modern-day Oman and the United Arab Emirates).

Its ability to survive extreme desert conditions revolutionized trade, transport, and warfare

- Nomadic tribes of the Arabian Peninsula
- Nabataean Kingdom
- [[Babylonians]]
- [[Assyrians]]
- [[Persians]]
- [[Mansa Musa]]' empire
