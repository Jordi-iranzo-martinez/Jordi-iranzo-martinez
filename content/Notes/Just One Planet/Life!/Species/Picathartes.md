Picathartes (commonly known as rockfowl or bald crows) is a genus of two extraordinary, ancient passerine bird species native to the closed-canopy tropical rainforests of West and Central [[Web/content/Notes/Just One Planet/Life!/Wonderful Places/Africa]]. 
*[[Congo Basin]], [[Birds]]*

Forming their own monogeneric family, Picathartidae, these medium-sized, ground-dwelling birds are famous for their brightly colored, completely featherless heads, crow-like bills, and habit of building mud nests inside caves and beneath rock overhangs.
