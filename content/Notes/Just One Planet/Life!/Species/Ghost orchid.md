
The ghost orchid (*Dendrophylax lindenii*) is an exceptionally rare, endangered, and ephemeral perennial orchid. It is native entirely to the saturated, humid hardwood hammocks and deep cypress swamps of southern [[Florida]], [[Cuba]], and the Bahamas.

It is widely celebrated in the botanical world as one of the most enigmatic, phantom-like flowers on Earth due to its unique evolutionary biology:

The plant earned its name because it is an epiphytic, completely leafless orchid.
