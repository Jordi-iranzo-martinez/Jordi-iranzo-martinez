
The bumblebee (genus Bombus) is a large, hairy, and highly social insect belonging to the apid bee family (Apidae) ([[Web/content/Notes/Just One Planet/Life!/Taxonomy/Zoology/Insects|Insects]])

Found primarily in temperate, alpine, and high-latitude regions of the Northern Hemisphere, bumblebees are easily recognized by their plump bodies, loud buzzing flight, and vibrant black-and-yellow warning bands.
