The Moray Eel (family Muraenidae) is a large, predatory marine fish known for its elongated, snake-like body and formidable jaws.

With approximately 200 species distributed globally, they are a dominant presence in tropical and temperate coral reefs, rocky shores, and deep-sea environments.
