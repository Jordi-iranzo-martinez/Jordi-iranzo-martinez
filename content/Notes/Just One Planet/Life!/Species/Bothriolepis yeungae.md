*Bothriolepis yeungae* is an extinct placoderm of the Late [[Devonian]] period (approximately 360 to 370 million years ago).

The vast majority of what scientists know about Bothriolepis yeungae comes from a spectacular archaeological find near Canowindra, New South Wales, [[Australia]].
