
Gastroticha (from Greek *gaster* "stomach", and *thrix* "hair")


### Gastroticha taonomy

- **Macrodasyida** (Marine): exclusively marine or estuarine species characterized by an elongated, strap-like body shape and numerous adhesive tubes distributed along the sides of their body.

- **Chaetonotida** (freshwater & marine gastrotrichs): the largest group, containing mostly freshwater species with a distinct tenpin or bottle-shaped body, featuring only a single pair of posterior adhesive tubes
