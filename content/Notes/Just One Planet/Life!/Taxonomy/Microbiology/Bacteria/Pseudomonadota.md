[[Bacteria]]

- **Alphaproteobacteria**: Includes nitrogen-fixing bacteria that live in symbiosis with plants (e.g., _Rhizobium_) and the evolutionary ancestors of eukaryotic mitochondria.

- **Betaproteobacteria**: Includes ammonia-oxidizing bacteria and pathogens like _Neisseria_ (causing meningitis and gonorrhea).

- **Gammaproteobacteria**: The largest class. It includes famous enteric bacteria like _Escherichia coli_ (_E. coli_), _Salmonella_, and deep-sea quimiolithotrophs found near hydrothermal vents and brine lakes.

- **Deltaproteobacteria**: Primarily contains sulfate-reducing bacteria and unique predatory bacteria like _Bdellovibrio_.

- **Epsilonproteobacteria**: Often found in extreme environments and includes human stomach pathogens like _Helicobacter pylori_.
