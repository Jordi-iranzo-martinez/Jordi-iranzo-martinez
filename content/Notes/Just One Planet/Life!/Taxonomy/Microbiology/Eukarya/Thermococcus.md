
*Thermococcus* is a genus of hyperthermophilic and extremely resilient archaea that inhabit some of the hottest and most hostile environments on the planet, such as deep-sea hydrothermal vents and submarine volcanoes.

[[Archaea]]

They thrive in an astonishing temperature range, generally between 70°C and 95°C (158°F to 203°F), with some species easily tolerating temperatures exceeding 100°C without their cells breaking down.

They live in the absolute darkness of the ocean floor where oxygen is nonexistent. Their metabolism is based on the fermentation of amino acids and sugars, using elemental sulfur to generate energy.

Many *Thermococcus* species possess a large cluster of flagella (whip-like tails) at one end of their body, allowing them to swim with great speed and force through the dense, hot water currents near volcanic vents.
