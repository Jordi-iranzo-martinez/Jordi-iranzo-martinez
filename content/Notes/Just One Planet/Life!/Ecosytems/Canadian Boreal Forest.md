
[[Canada]]

The Canadian boreal forest (or taiga) is a vast, continuous band of northern woodland, lakes, and wetlands stretching across nearly 60% of Canada's land area—from Newfoundland and Labrador on the Atlantic coast to the Yukon and northern British Columbia in the west. Covering roughly 552 million hectares (including over 300 million hectares of forest), it represents nearly one-third of the world's circumpolar boreal zone and is the largest intact forest and wetland ecosystem left on Earth.
