
Didier Dinamou is a energy engineer from [Camaeroon](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Cameroon/@7.3696175,12.2940041,1599225m/data=!3m2!1e3!4b1!4m6!3m5!1s0x10613753703e0f21:0x2b03c44599829b53!8m2!3d7.369722!4d12.354722!16zL20vMDFubG4?entry=ttu&g_ep=EgoyMDI2MTExMC4wIKXMDSoASAFQAw%3D%3D) and founder of the startup Diwa Innovation, internationally recognized for inventing DinaBreeze, a low-cost, eco-friendly air-cooling system made from terracotta clay and powered by solar energy.

He earned a degree in renewable energy engineering (specializing in solar energy) from the National Advanced School of Engineering at the University of Maroua in Cameroon's Far North region, followed by a master's degree in innovative materials.

He originally developed the prototype as his graduation project to provide thermal comfort in the Sahel region, where extreme heat coincides with limited access to the electrical grid.

He creted DinaBreeze, a device that combines locally sourced traditional materials with basic photovoltaic technology to cool homes without relying on grid electricity or polluting refrigerant gases:

It uses moistened porous clay tubes through which air is circulated by a fan. As water evaporates from the terracotta surface, it absorbs ambient heat and releases cool air into the room (adiabatic cooling).

In tests conducted in hot, dry climates, the unit has lowered indoor temperatures by over 10 °C—dropping room temperatures from 36 °C to 25 °C, and even keeping 30 m² rooms at 25 °C when outdoor temperatures approach 48 °C.

For the social and environmental impact of his innovation, Dinamou has received several African entrepreneurship and technology awards, notably winning first prize in the Central African stage of the EDF Pulse Africa Tour (2023), alongside recognition from TotalEnergies Startupper and Orange POESAM.
