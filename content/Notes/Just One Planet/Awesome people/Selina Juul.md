Selina Juul (born March 7, 1980) is a Russian-Danish activist, graphic designer, and internationally recognized authority on reducing food waste. She is the founder and Chairwoman of the Board of Stop Wasting Food (Stop Spild Af Mad), Denmark's largest non-profit consumer movement against food waste.

Born in Moscow, Soviet Union, Juul grew up experiencing severe food shortages following the collapse of communism. When she emigrated to Denmark at age 13, she was shocked by the scale of edible food discarded in supermarkets and households.

In 2008, while working as a graphic designer, she launched a simple Facebook group called *[Stop Spild Af Mad](https://stopspildafmad.org/)*. Rather than blaming or shaming consumers and retailers, she focused on practical solutions that save both money and the environment.

Through the [Stop Wasting Food movement](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAESfAHEIkXXlag4_nyufMWVCmmqg8IY_aNs07e-WvGT31aK3o8Tfi_iwSoHuDPnWjdfRFAZKONH2xGCA0rrBqQ_vOkgkvfL9_2K0XQ7XbjZrIq1ByZJOQiPLC0RoAUb_MLeqRaSdIXll5XcQyG5cQDLtJU0SsvWgtDa_myTxNA), Juul helped spark a nationwide shift in how Denmark handles food surplus.

In 2008, she successfully persuaded the major Danish supermarket chain REMA 1000 to abolish multi-buy quantity discounts (such as "buy three for the price of two") in favor of single-item discounts—a policy later adopted by other chains to prevent shoppers from overbuying.

Her campaigns, partnerships with the food industry, and educational initiatives contributed to Denmark reducing its national food waste by roughly 25% between 2010 and 2015.

She also conceived GratisMad.dk (Free Food Portal), launched in 2021 to connect food-insecure families, refugees, and vulnerable groups with local surplus food initiatives across Denmark, and authored the bestselling leftovers cookbook _Stop spild af mad: En kogebog med mere_.

She serves as a member of the global coalition [Champions 12.3](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAESaAHEIkXX8p-Awox8E2ty-mn2K2a3F6r6u0hGVVHz3Pr1P2rY1fDJcKKxNc2ICmcI7Ktja9A8wLdtq5-zyK1-y8lmx9YAaM8EvG_XiuN_NNUjVqn6ZNEzSQLmmbYpre1yTm37j4hnGLQb), the EU Platform on Food Losses and Food Waste, and the UN FAO's SAVE FOOD initiative.

She has received numerous awards, including the Nordic Council Nature and Environment Prize (2013), Dane of the Year (2014), and Reader's Digest European of the Year (2020).
