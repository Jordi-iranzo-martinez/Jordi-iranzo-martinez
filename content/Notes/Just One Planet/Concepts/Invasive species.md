
Invasive species

An invasive species is a non-native (introduced) organism—such as a plant, animal, fungus, or microbe—that establishes itself in a new ecosystem and spreads rapidly, causing harm to native biodiversity, the economy, or human health.

Not all non-native species become invasive; to earn the label, an organism must outcompete native wildlife, disrupt ecosystem balances, or damage infrastructure and agriculture.
