

In biology and medicine, sex refers to the biological and physiological traits that define male, female, and intersex organisms [1.4]. In human beings, biological sex is a complex system determined by a combination of genetics, chromosomes, hormones, and anatomical structures, playing a fundamental role in reproduction, evolution, and overall health.
