Human beings (Homo sapiens) are a species of highly intelligent primates characterized by their bipedalism, advanced cognitive abilities, complex language systems, and deep capacity for introspection. They are the only surviving members of the genus Homo and have successfully adapted to almost every ecosystem on Earth.
