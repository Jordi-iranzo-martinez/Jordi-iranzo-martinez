TOMRA Systems ASA is a Norwegian multinational technology company headquartered in Asker, Norway, that designs and manufactures sensor-based collection and sorting machines used worldwide for recycling, waste management, food processing, and mining.

Founded in 1972 by brothers Tore and Petter Planke after inventing the world's first fully automated bottle-return machine, TOMRA operates across several major segments:

- **TOMRA Collection** (Reverse Vending). Best known for its Reverse Vending Machines (RVMs)—the automated machines in supermarkets where consumers insert empty plastic bottles, aluminum cans, and glass bottles to receive deposit refunds (Deposit Return Schemes / _SDDR_). Over 90,000 TOMRA RVMs are installed globally.

- **TOMRA Recycling**. Develops high-speed optical and AI-powered sorting systems (such as _AUTOSORT™_ and _GAINnext™_) that identify and separate plastics, paper, wood, and scrap metals from mixed waste streams using near-infrared, X-ray, and visual sensors.

- **TOMRA Food**. Uses optical sensors, lasers, and cameras to inspect, grade, and sort fresh and processed foods (such as fruit, potatoes, nuts, and vegetables) by ripeness, size, color, and defects—reducing food waste and improving food safety.

- **TOMRA Mining**. Applies X-ray transmission (XRT) and laser sensor technology to separate valuable ores and gemstones (like diamonds, copper, and tungsten) from waste rock with significantly lower water and energy consumption.
