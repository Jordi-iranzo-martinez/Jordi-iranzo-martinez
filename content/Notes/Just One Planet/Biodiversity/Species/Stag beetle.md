The stag beetle (*Lucanus cervus*) is the biggest beatle on Europe ([[Web/content/Notes/Just One Planet/Biodiversity/Taxonomy/Zoology/Insects|Insects]]), from 3 to 9 cm long. Males are bigger and have a huge red mandibles which look like deer antlers.
