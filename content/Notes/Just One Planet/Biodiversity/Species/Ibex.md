An ibex is a species of wild mountain goat belonging to the genus *Capra*, famous for its huge curved horns and extraordinary rock-climbing ability.

[[Goats]]

Their split, concave hooves have a soft inner pad that grips narrow rock ledges and near-vertical cliff faces.

Both males and females grow horns, though males (_bucks_) develop dramatic, backward-curving horns lined with ridges that can grow over 1 meter (3+ feet) long.

They live in steep, rocky alpine regions above the tree line across [[Europe]], North [[Africa]], and [[Asia]].

*[[High Mountains]]*


Main Species

- **Iberian Ibex** (*Capra pyrenaica*). Native to [[Spain]], also known as the Spanish ibex or _cabra montés_.

- **Alpine Ibex** (_Capra ibex_). Native to the European Alps, easily recognized by its thick brown coat and heavily ridged scimitar-shaped horns.

- **Nubian & Siberian Ibex**. Desert- and high-altitude-adapted species found across the Middle East, North Africa, and Central Asia.
