
Medicinal leeches (primarily *Hirudo medicinalis* and closely related species like *Hirudo verbana*) are freshwater segmented worms (annelids) used in medicine for over 3,000 years.

While historically associated with bloodletting, sterile, lab-bred medicinal leeches are FDA-approved medical devices used today in reconstructive microsurgery.
