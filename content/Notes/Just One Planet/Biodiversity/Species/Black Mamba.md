
The black mamba (*Dendroaspis polylepis*) is a species of highly venomous snake belonging to the family Elapidae, native to parts of sub-Saharan Africa. It is the longest venomous snake in Africa and among the fastest-moving snakes in the world.

The snake's scales are not black; they range from olive and yellowish-brown to gunmetal gray, with a pale cream or greenish-white underbelly. Its name comes from the blue-black to inky-black coloration inside its mouth, which it displays as a warning sign when threatened.

Adults typically measure between 2 and 2.5 meters (6.6 to 8.2 feet) in length, though exceptional specimens can reach over 4 meters. Slender and muscular, it can travel at bursts of up to 11–16 km/h (7–10 mph) over short distances.
