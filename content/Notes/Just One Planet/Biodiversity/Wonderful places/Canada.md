
Canada is a North American country consisting of 10 provinces and 3 territories, stretching from the Atlantic Ocean to the Pacific Ocean and northward into the Arctic Ocean.

At nearly 9.98 million square kilometers (3.855 million square miles), it is the second-largest country in the world by total area and possesses the longest coastline globally.

The federal capital is Ottawa. Its largest metropolitan areas are Toronto (the financial hub), Montreal (the largest Francophone city in the Americas), and Vancouver.

As of 2026, Canada's population is approximately 41.8 million, with the vast majority living within a narrow corridor near the southern border with the United States

Canada is a federal parliamentary democracy and a constitutional monarchy. King Charles III.

Both English and French are official federal languages, with French serving as the sole official provincial language in Quebec.
