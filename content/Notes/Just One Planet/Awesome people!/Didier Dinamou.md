
Didier Dinamou is an energy engineer and inventor from [Camaeroon](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Cameroon/@7.3696175,12.2940041,1599225m/data=!3m2!1e3!4b1!4m6!3m5!1s0x10613753703e0f21:0x2b03c44599829b53!8m2!3d7.369722!4d12.354722!16zL20vMDFubG4?entry=ttu&g_ep=EgoyMDI2MTExMC4wIKXMDSoASAFQAw%3D%3D), founder of the startup Diwa Innovation (also cited as D.Y. Innovation).

The company i internationally recognized for developing DinaBreeze, a low-cost, eco-friendly cooling system made from terracotta, water, and solar energy.
