Hunting

1. **The Hardest Challenge**: An overview of predator-prey dynamics, showing that most hunting attempts actually end in failure.
2. **In the Grip of the Seasons** (Arctic): Explores how Arctic predators like polar snails and arctic foxes must constantly alter their hunting tactics as the ice melts.
3. **Hide and Seek** (Forests): Focuses on hunting in dense jungles and woodlands where visibility is highly limited, forcing predators like tigers and chimpanzees to rely on stealth.
4. **Hunger at Sea** (Oceans): Examines the open ocean—a vast blue desert where predators like blue whales and frigatebirds must travel immense distances to locate food.
5. **Nowhere to Hide** (Plains): Dedicated to open grasslands and deserts where prey can spot danger from miles away, making speed and teamwork essential for cheetahs and lions.
6. **Race Against Time** (Coasts): Analyzes the shifting worlds of coastlines where tidal windows force marine and land predators to strike quickly before the water rolls back.
7. **Living with Predators** (Conservation): A final, poignant episode looking at the conservation efforts required to protect the world's remaining apex predators in a human-dominated world.
