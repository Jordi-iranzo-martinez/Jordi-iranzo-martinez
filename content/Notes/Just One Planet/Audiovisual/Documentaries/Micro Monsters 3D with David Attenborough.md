Using custom-built macroscopic 3D camera rigs, the series shrinks viewers down to bug-size to explore the hidden, alien-like lives of terrestrial arthropods—insects, spiders, and scorpions—which account for over 80% of all known animal species on Earth.

*[[Arachnids]] and [[Web/content/Notes/Just One Planet/Biodiversity/Taxonomy/Zoology/Insects|Insects]]*

The original 2013 series consists of six 22-to-25-minute thematic episodes, accompanied by a behind-the-scenes special:

1. **Conflict** — Explores how arthropods survive against rival bugs and giant predators using armor, camouflage, mimicry, poison, and chemical weapons.

2. **Predator** — Examines specialized hunting strategies, featuring the intelligent Portia jumping spider, whirligig beetles, and emerald cockroach wasps.

3. **Courtship** — Reveals elaborate mating rituals, from acrobatic aerial dances to pheromone signaling and perilous gifts.

4. **Reproduction** — Follows life cycles, metamorphosis, and unusual reproductive strategies, including species that reproduce without mating.

5. **Family** — Looks at parental care in the arthropod world, from scorpions carrying young on their backs to protective mothers guarding eggs.

6. **Colony** — Dives into complex superorganisms, showing how millions of ants, bees, and termites cooperate to build towering castles and wage coordinated wars.

- **The Making of Micro Monsters** — A behind-the-scenes look at the custom 3D macro lenses, motion-control rigs, and studio sets built across three continents to film tiny subjects at close range.

Standard 3D cameras are spaced too far apart to film tiny insects without distorting perspective. The production team engineered specialized mirrors and miniature twin-lens macro rigs to capture crisp, true-to-scale stereoscopic 3D footage.

In 2020, Alchemy Immersive and Atlantic Productions released a separate, Emmy-nominated 5-part 180° 3D virtual reality series narrated by Attenborough for Oculus Quest headsets, filmed in 8K at 60fps with spatial audio.
