Attenborough and the Sea Dragon is about an [[Ichthyosaurs]] fossil on the Britain's famous [[Web/content/Notes/Jurassic]] Coast in Dorset.

The program follows a team of paleontologists and fossil preparators as they excavate, CT-scan, and forensically investigate the remarkably preserved 200-million-year-old skeleton of an ichthyosaur—an extinct, dolphin-like marine reptile nicknamed a "sea dragon."
