Gorillas in the Mist refers to both primatologist Dian Fossey's landmark 1983 memoir and the acclaimed 1988 biographical drama film starring Sigourney Weaver.

Both chronicle Fossey's groundbreaking 18-year field study of endangered mountain gorillas in the [[Virunga]] Mountains of Rwanda and her fierce, ultimately fatal battle against poachers.

 Inspired by anthropologist Louis Leakey in 1966, Fossey travels to Central Africa to conduct a census of vanishing mountain gorillas. 
 
*[[Gorilla]]*

 Establishing the Karisoke Research Center in Rwanda, she gains the unprecedented trust of several gorilla groups—most notably a young silverback named Digit. As poachers slaughter gorillas for trophies and illegal zoo sales, Fossey's conservation work escalates into an obsessive, militant campaign against poachers and corrupt officials, culminating in her unsolved murder at her cabin in December 1985.
