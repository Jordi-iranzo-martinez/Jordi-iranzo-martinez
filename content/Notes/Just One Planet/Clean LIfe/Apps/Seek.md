Seek by iNaturalist is a free mobile nature-identification app created by the iNaturalist team—a joint initiative of the [California Academy of Sciences](https://www.calacademy.org/) and the [National Geographic Society](https://www.nationalgeographic.com/).

*[[California]]*

Designed for families, students, hikers, and casual nature enthusiasts, it uses computer vision (AI image recognition) trained on millions of verified iNaturalist observations to identify plants, animals, and fungi in real time through a smartphone camera.

Pointing your phone's camera at a plant, insect, bird, mushroom, or shell triggers on-screen taxonomic narrowing—moving from broad groups (like kingdom or family) down to the exact species as the camera gets a clearer angle.

Because the machine-learning model is built directly into the app, Seek can identify species even in remote forests, mountains, or parks without cellular data or Wi-Fi.

To encourage outdoor exploration, users earn digital badges for discovering different taxonomic groups (such as amphibians, arachnids, or wildflowers) and completing monthly seasonal observation challenges.

Unlike the main iNaturalist platform, Seek does **not** require user registration or an account, does not collect personal data, and never shares your exact GPS location publicly (using broad regional data only to suggest locally common species).
