Sleep is a naturally recurring, reversible state of mind and body characterized by altered consciousness, inhibited sensory activity, and reduced muscle movement. It is a vital biological necessity for human beings, acting as the body's primary mechanism for neurological restoration, metabolic regulation, and overall health.

[[Health]]

Sleep is not a uniform state of rest but a dynamic cycle divided into two main categories that repeat every 90 to 110 minutes:


- **Non-REM (NREM) Sleep:** Divided into three stages. Stage 1 and 2 are light sleep. Stage 3 is **Deep Sleep** (Slow-Wave Sleep), which is the most physically restorative phase. During deep sleep, the body repairs tissues, builds bone and muscle, and strengthens the immune system.

- **REM (Rapid Eye Movement) Sleep:** The phase where intense dreaming occurs. The brain becomes highly active—almost identical to awake states—while muscles are temporarily paralyzed. REM sleep is critical for emotional processing, memory consolidation, and mental clarity, heavily influencing daily **happiness** and cognitive functions.
