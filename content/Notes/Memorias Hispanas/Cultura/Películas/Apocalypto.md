### The Maya civilization



It is set in the Yucatán Peninsula during the declining period of the Maya empire, just before the arrival of Spanish conquistadors. To maintain historical authenticity, the entire film is spoken in the native Yucatec Maya language with subtitles

In ancient Maya society, the _Holcánes_ were a highly organized elite warrior class. Many were nobles who trained their entire lives for warfare. They protected their city-states, enforced laws, and fought in strategic battles against rival cities.

To the Maya, blood was considered the ultimate spiritual nourishment for the gods. Sacrificing a life was the highest possible offering to maintain cosmic balance, bring rain, or heal diseases

The Maya typically sacrificed high-status elite captives (captured rival kings, generals, and enemy nobility) as they were considered more valuable to the gods.

Maya sacrifices were small, highly specific, and deeply ritualistic. There is no archaeological evidence of mass slaughter fields.

Their most common and sacred form of sacrifice was decapitation.

Maya kings and queens regularly pierced their own tongues, earlobes, or skin with stingray spines or obsidian blades to offer their own royal blood to the gods during major festivals


The Spanish arrived in the Yucatán Peninsula around 1517. The Maya civilization with giant, bustling stone citieshad already collapsed and been abandoned around 900 AD.

There were no "uncontacted" forest tribes living right next to massive cities. Even rural villagers were part of the empire's economic network, traded goods (like salt, obsidian, and jade), paid taxes to the local rulers, and were well aware of urban life.

The elite Maya military relied heavily on *macuahuitl* (razor-sharp obsidian blades), specialized throwing *atlatls* (spears), and padded cotton armor soaked in saltwater to make it tough against arrows. Their gear was highly advanced for its time.
