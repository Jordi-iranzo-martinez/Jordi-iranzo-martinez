
Bacillaria is a historic genus of colonial pennate diatoms distinguished by their extraordinary, synchronized, sliding collective movements. They represent the very first formally described genus of diatoms in scientific history. Individual cells are joined side-by-side by a specialized elastic, actin-based microscopic track system along their raphes (cell wall slits), allowing the entire stacked colony to physically expand and contract in a rhythmic, accordion-like choreographed dance.
