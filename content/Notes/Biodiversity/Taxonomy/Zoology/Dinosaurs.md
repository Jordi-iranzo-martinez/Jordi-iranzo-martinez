Dinosaurs were a diverse group of reptiles that first appeared during the Triassic period (around 243 to 233 million years ago) and became the dominant terrestrial vertebrates for over 135 million years through the Jurassic and Cretaceous periods

### Triassic dinosarus

The first dinosaurs appeared during the Late Triassic, around 230 million years ago. They were small, agile, and lightweight, living in the shadow of massive crocodile-like reptiles that ruled the land.

- Herrerasaurus
- Eoraptor
