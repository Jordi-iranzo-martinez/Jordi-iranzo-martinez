
Pentastomida (from Greek *πέντε* "five" and *στόma* "mouth"), knon a tongue worms, [[Crustacea]]


### Pentastomida taxonomy

- Porocephalida: the largest order, characterized by a distinct division between the hook placement and the mouth. Their larvae primarily parasitize mammals or fish before reaching maturity in reptiles.

- **Cephalobaenida**: a more primitive order where the hooks are located directly on distinct abdominal lobes flanking the central mouth.

- **Raillietiellida**: Specialized tongue worms that primarily complete their adult lifecycle in the lungs of small lizards, geckos, and amphibians.

- **Reighardiida**: An unusual marine order that bypasses intermediate hosts, directly parasitizing the air sacs of seabirds (such as gulls and terns).
