
Acantocephala (from Greek *akantha* "thorn" or "spine", and *kephale*: "head" )



### Acantocephala taxonomy


- **Archiacanthocephala** (ancient): Parasites that primarily target terrestrial birds and mammals, utilizing insects or centipedes as intermediate hosts.

- **Eoacanthocephala** (primitive): Small parasites that predominantly infest aquatic cold-blooded vertebrates, such as marine and freshwater fishes, as well as turtles.

- **Palaeacanthocephala** (modern): The largest and most diverse class, parasitizing a wide range of aquatic birds, marine mammals, and fishes, utilizing crustaceans as intermediate hosts.

- **Polyacanthocephala** (Specialized): A small, highly specialized class containing a single genus (_Polyacanthorhynchus_) that infests freshwater fish and caimans in South America.
