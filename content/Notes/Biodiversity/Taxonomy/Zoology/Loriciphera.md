
Loriciphera (from Latin *lorica*: "armor" and _ferre_ "bring")



### Loriciphera taxonomy

- **Nanaloricidae**: rigid, heavily reinforced outer case (lorica) made of solid longitudinal plates and an elongated mouth cone.

- **Pliciloricidae**: highly flexible outer case featuring numerous vertical folds (plicae) rather than solid solid plates.

- **Urnaloricidae**: a small, highly specialized deep-sea family easily identified by its unique urn-shaped larval characteristics.
