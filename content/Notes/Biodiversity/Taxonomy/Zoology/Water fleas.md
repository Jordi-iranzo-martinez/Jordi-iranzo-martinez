
 Water fleas (*Daphnia* spp.) is a genus of small, planktonic [[Crustacea]]. Belonging to the order Cladocera, these translucent creatures are found across a vast range of freshwater environments—from large lakes to temporary backwater ponds—and serve as a crucial keystone species in aquatic food webs.
