
Vertebrata



### Vertebrata taonomy


- **[[Agnatha]]** (Jawless Fishes): The most primitive lineage of living vertebrates, completely lacking jaws, scales, and paired fins (e.g., hagfishes and lampreys).

- **[[Chondrichthyes]]** (Cartilaginous Fishes): Jawed marine vertebrates whose internal skeletons are made entirely of flexible cartilage instead of bone (e.g., sharks, rays, and chimaeras).

- **[[Actinopterygii]]** (Ray-finned Fishes): The largest group of aquatic vertebrates, characterized by bony skeletons and fins supported by thin, flexible bony rays (e.g., salmon, tuna, and clownfish).

- **[[Sarcopterygii]]** (Lobe-finned Fishes): Bony fishes with muscular, fleshy lobed fins that represent the direct evolutionary ancestors of all land-dwelling vertebrates (e.g., coelacanths and lungfishes).

- **[[Amphibians]]:** Ectothermic vertebrates that typically split their life cycle between an aquatic larval stage and a terrestrial adult stage (e.g., frogs, toads, and salamanders

- **[[Reptiles]]**: Amniote vertebrates covered in horny scales or scutes that lay amniotic eggs, heavily adapted to terrestrial survival (e.g., snakes, lizards, crocodilians, and turtles).

- **[[Birds]]**: Endothermic, feathered avian dinosaurs equipped with highly kinetic skulls, toothless beaks, and wings adapted for powered flight.

- **[[Biodiversity/Taxonomy/Zoology/Mammals|Mammals]]**: Advanced endothermic vertebrates characterized by the possession of mammary glands that produce milk, body hair or fur, and three specialized middle ear bones.
