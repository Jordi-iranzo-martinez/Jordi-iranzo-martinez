
Sipunculida


### Sipunculida taxonomy

- **Sipunculidea** (True Peanut Worms): characterized by possessing tentacles arranged in a complete circle surrounding the mouth, or clustered on a specialized dorsal structure. They are mostly deposit feeders in muddy sediments.

- **Phascolosomatidea** (Banded Peanut Worms): characterized by having tentacles arranged in an arc or horseshoe-shaped pattern above the mouth, rather than a full circle, often featuring distinct dark epidermal bands.
