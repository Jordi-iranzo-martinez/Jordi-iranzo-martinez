

Brachiopoda





### Brachiopoda taonomy

- **Linguliformea** (Inarticulate Chitinous): Primitive brachiopods with shells made of calcium phosphate and chitin that lack a mechanical hinge, relying entirely on complex musculature to open and close their valves.

- **Craniiformea** (Inarticulate Calcareous): A small group of inarticulate brachiopods with calcareous shells that completely lack a pedicle, instead cementing themselves directly onto hard rocky substrates.

- **Rhynchonelliformea** (articulate): The largest and most diverse lineage, characterized by calcium carbonate shells securely interlocked by a specialized tooth-and-socket hinge mechanism.
