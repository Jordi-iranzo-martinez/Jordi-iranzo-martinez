
Cnidaria (from Gree κνίδη: "stinging nettle")


### Cnidaria taxonomy

- **Anthozoa** (corals & sea anemones): the largest class, entirely lacking a medusa stage and existing exclusively as polyps that often build massive calcium carbonate reefs.

- **Scyphozoa** (true jellyfish): open-ocean swimmers where the large, umbrella-shaped medusa stage dominates the life cycle, while the polyp stage is minute.

- **Cubozoa** (box jellyfish / sea Wasps): agile, powerful swimmers with distinctly square-shaped bodies, complex lens-bearing eyes, and highly potent, lethal venoms.

- **Hydrozoa** (hydroids & siphonophores): a highly diverse group that forms complex, polymorphic colonies of specialized individuals, such as the Portuguese man-o'-war.

- **Staurozoa** (stalked jellyfish): unusual, trumpet-shaped cold-water jellyfish that spend their lives anchored upside down to rocks or seaweed via a specialized stalk.
