Rhinos (family Rhinocerotidae) are massive megaherbivores easily recognized by their prominent horns and thick, armor-like ski They are among the largest remaining land mammals, but today, they face a severe survival crisis.

Rhinos

- **Ceratotherium**: ncludes the White Rhinoceros (_Ceratotherium simum_), which is split into the Near Threatened Southern white rhino and the functionally extinct Northern white rhino.

- **Diceros**: Includes the Critically Endangered Black Rhinoceros (_Diceros bicornis_) native to eastern and southern Africa.

- **Rhinoceros**: Includes the Vulnerable Greater One-Horned Rhinoceros (_Rhinoceros unicornis_) and the Critically Endangered Javan Rhinoceros (_Rhinoceros sondaicus_), both possessing only a single horn.

- **Dicerorhinus**: Includes the Critically Endangered **Sumatran Rhinoceros** (_Dicerorhinus sumatrensis_), the smallest living rhino and the only one covered in long, shaggy hair.
