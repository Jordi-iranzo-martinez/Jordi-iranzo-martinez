
Tardigrada (from Latin *tardus* "slo" y _gradus_ "paso") also known as "water bears" are microscopic, eight-legged invertebrates celebrated as the most resilient animals on Earth. 






### Tardigrada taonomy

- **Heterotardigrada** (armored): possessing hard cuticular plates that act as armor on their backs and complex sensory appendages on their head region.

- **Eutardigrada** (naked): having a smooth, flexible cuticle that completely lacks hard armor plates, featuring simplified claws on their legs.
