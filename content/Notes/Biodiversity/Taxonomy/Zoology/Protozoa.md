
Protozoa (from Greek proto ancient and oo animal)




### Protozoa taxonomy

- **Amoebozoa** (Amoebas / Rhizopods): Unicellular organisms that move and capture food by extending temporary, fluid-like projections of their cytoplasm called pseudopodia (false feet).

-  **Ciliophora** (Ciliates): Advanced protozoans covered in hundreds of microscopic, hair-like structures called cilia that beat rhythmically for rapid swimming and feeding (e.g., Paramecium).

- **Flagellates** (Mastigophora): Free-living or parasitic protozoans equipped with one or more whip-like tails called flagella used for propulsion (e.g., Euglena and Trypanosoma).

- **Apicomplexa** (Sporozoans): Strictly parasitic, non-motile protozoans characterized by complex lifecycles and a specialized apical structure used to penetrate host cells (e.g., Plasmodium, the parasite responsible for malaria).
