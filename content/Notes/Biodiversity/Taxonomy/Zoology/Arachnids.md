


### Arachnids taxonomy

- **Araneae** (Spiders)
- **Scorpiones** (Scorpions)
- **Opiliones** (Harvestmen)
- **Pseudoscorpiones** (Pseudoscorpions)
- **Solifugae**: Camel spiders, Wind scorpions)
- **Amblypygi**: Whip spiders, Tailless whip scorpions)
- **Thelyphonida** (Uropygi): Vinegaroons, Whip scorpions
- **Schizomida**: Short-tailed whip scorpions
- **Palpigradi**: Microwhip scorpions
- **Ricinulei**: Hooded tickspiders
- **Acari**: Mites and Ticks
