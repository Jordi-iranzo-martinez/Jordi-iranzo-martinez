
The Devonian Sea (spanning from 419 to 358 million years ago during the Paleozoic Era) is famously known among scientists as the "Age of Fishes" because marine ecosystems experienced an unprecedented explosion of fish diversity and jawed predators.





[[Fishes]]

- Dunkleosteus (*Dunkleosteus terrelli*)
- Gogo fish (*Mcnamaraspis kaprios*)
- *Groenlandaspis* sp.
- *Bothriolepis yeungae*
- *Remigolepis* sp.
- *Mandageria fairfaxi*
- *Acanthostega gunnari*  
- *Tiktaalik roseae*
