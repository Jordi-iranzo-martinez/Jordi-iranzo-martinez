### Paleozoic Periods

- **Cambrian** (541–485 million years ago): Famous for the Cambrian Explosion, a rapid evolutionary event where most major animal phyla first appeared in the oceans.

- **Ordovician** (485–443 million years ago): Characterized by a massive diversification of marine life, ending in Earth's first major mass extinction event.

- **Silurian** (443–419 million years ago): Marked the stabilization of Earth's climate and the first appearance of vascular plants and jawed fish.

- **Devonian** (419–358 million years ago): Known as the "Age of Fishes" due to the rise of massive marine predators, alongside the first tetrapods moving onto land.

- **Carboniferous** (358–298 million years ago): Famous for vast, swampy forests that formed Earth's modern coal deposits, as well as giant insects and the evolution of amniotic eggs.

- **Permian** (298–252 million years ago): The continents merged into the supercontinent Pangaea, ending with the "Great Dying," the most severe mass extinction in Earth's history.
