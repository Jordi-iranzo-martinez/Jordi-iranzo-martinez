



### Mesozoic Period

- **Triassic** (252–201 million years ago): Life slowly recovered from the Permian extinction, seeing the rise of the very first dinosaurs and early mammals.

- **Jurassic** (201–145 million years ago): Dinosaurs became the dominant land animals, lush gymnosperm forests flourished, and the first birds evolved.

- **Cretaceous** (145–66 million years ago): Marked by the rise of flowering plants and iconic dinosaurs like the _T. rex_, ending with the Chicxulub asteroid impact that wiped out non-avian dinosaurs.
