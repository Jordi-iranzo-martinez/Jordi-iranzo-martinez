
Bacteria is a massive domain of single-celled, prokaryotic microorganisms that lack a membrane-bound nucleus and true organelles




### Bacteria taxonomy


- Deep-Branching Extremophiles & Thermophiles

	- **[[Aquifex]]** (Aquificota): Hyperthermophilic, rod-shaped bacteria found in deep-sea vents that represent some of the oldest living lineages.
	
	- **[[Thermodesulfobacterium]]** (Thermodesulfobacteriota): Thermophilic, sulfate-reducing bacteria that thrive in extreme hot springs and oil reservoirs.
	
	- **[[Thermotoga]]** (Thermotogae): Unique anaerobic thermophiles enclosed in a balloon-like outer membrane sheath known as a "toga."
	
	- **[[Deinococci]]** (Deinococcota): Famous extremophiles renowned for their unparalleled resistance to lethal doses of ionizing radiation and dehydration.

- Photosynthetic & Autotrophic Lineages

	- **[[Chloroflexi]]** (Green Non-Sulfur Bacteria): filamentous bacteria that utilize anoxygenic gliding photosynthesis, often found in hot spring microbial mats.
	
	- **[[Chlorobi]]** (Green Sulfur Bacteria): Strictly anaerobic photoautotrophs that use sulfur compounds instead of water during photosynthesis.
	
	- **[[Cyanobacteria]]** (Blue-Green Algae): Oxygenic photosynthetic bacteria that oxygenated Earth's early atmosphere, giving evolutionary rise to the **Chloroplast** organelle.

- The FCB & PVC Groups

	- **[[Flavobacteria]]** (Bacteroidota): Highly abundant marine and freshwater bacteria known for their rapid gliding motility and degradation of organic polymers.
	
	- **[[Cytophaga]]** (Bacteroidota): Soil and aquatic specialized bacteria proficient in breaking down tough plant cellulose and chitin.
	
	- **[[Planctomyces]]** / **[[Pirella]]** (Planctomycetota): Aquatic budding bacteria unique for lacking peptidoglycan walls and possessing complex internal membrane compartmentalization.
	
	- **[[Verrucomicrobia]]** (Verrucomicrobiota): Widespread aquatic and soil bacteria featuring distinct, wart-like cell surface protrusions.
	
	- **[[Chlamydia]]** (Chlamydiota): Obligate intracellular parasites that must live inside host cells, acting as major human and animal pathogens.

- Distinct & Gram-Positive Phyla

	- **[[Spirochetes]]** (Spirochaetota): Visually striking, helically coiled bacteria that move using internal axial filaments (e.g., the cause of Lyme disease).
	
	- **[[Deferribacter]]** (Deferribacterota): Anaerobic, rod-shaped aquatic bacteria that utilize iron, manganese, or nitrate as terminal electron acceptors.
	
	- **[[Nitrospira]]** (Nitrospirota): Chemolithoautotrophic nitrite-oxidizing bacteria that play a critical, dominant role in global nitrogen cycles.
	
	- **[[Actinobacteria]]** (High-GC Gram-Positives): Filamentous, fungus-like soil bacteria that serve as the primary source for modern medical antibiotics.
	
	- **[[Gram-positive bacteria]]** (Low-GC / Firmicutes): Thick-walled bacteria capable of forming highly resilient endospores (e.g., _Bacillus_ and _Clostridium_).
	
	- **[[Proteobacteria]]** (Gram-Negative Bacteria): The largest, most metabolically diverse phylum of bacteria, whose ancestral lines formed the evolutionary origin of the **Mitochondrion** organelle.
