


### Mammals taxonomy

- Prototheria
	- **Monotremata**: platypus and echidnas
- Theria
	- [[Metatheria]] (Marsupials)
		
	- Eutheria
		- **Rodentia**: mice, rats, squirrels, capybaras, and beavers
		- **Chiroptera**: bats
		- **Primates**: monkeys and apes
		- **Carnivora**: cats, dogs, bears, weasels, seals...
		- **Cetartiodactyla**: cows, pigs, deer, camels, hippopotamuses, whales, and dolphins
		- **Perissodactyla**: horses, zebras, rhinos, and tapires
		- **Proboscidea**: elephants
		- **Eulipotyphla**: Shrews, moles, and hedgehogs
		- **Sirenia**: manatees and dugongs
		- **Xenarthrans**: sloths, anteaters & armadillos
