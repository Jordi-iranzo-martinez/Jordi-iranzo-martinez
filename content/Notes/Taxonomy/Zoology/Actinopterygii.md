



### Actinopterygii taxonomy

- Cladistia
	- **Polypteriformes**: Bichirs and reedfishes (native to African freshwater systems).
- Chondrostei
	- **Acipenseriformes**: Sturgeons (famous for caviar) and paddlefishes.
- Neopterygii
	- Holostei
		- **Lepisosteiformes**: Gars (elongated bodies with needle-like teeth).
		- **Amiiformes**: Bowfins
	- Teleostei
		- **Anguilliformes**: True eels and morays (snake-like bodies).
		- **Cypriniformes**: Carps, minnows, loaches, and goldfishes.
		- **Siluriformes**: Catfishes (prominent sensory barbels or "whiskers").
		- **Salmoniformes**: Salmons, trouts, and chars.
		- **Gadiformes**: Cods, haddocks, and hakes.
		- **Syngnathiformes**: Seahorses, pipefishes, and dragonets.
		- **Scombriformes:** Tunas, mackerels, and bonitos.
		- **Pleuronectiformes**: Flatfishes (flounders, soles, and turbots; famous for having both eyes on one side of their head).
		- **Tetraodontiformes**: Pufferfishes, boxfishes, and triggerfishes.
		- **Perciformes**: Perches, cichlids, groupers, and wrasses (historically the largest order of all vertebrates).
