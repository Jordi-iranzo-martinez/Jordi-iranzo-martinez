Caudata taonomy

- **Cryptobranchoidea** (primitive Salamanders): A suborder containing giant aquatic species that exhibit external fertilization and retain primitive skeletal structures (e.g., Hellbenders and Giant Salamanders).

- **Salamandroidea** (Advanced Salamanders): The largest and most diverse suborder, utilizing internal fertilization via spermatophores, containing terrestrial, semi-aquatic, and fully aquatic newts and lungless salamanders.

- **Sirenoidea** (Sirens): Fully aquatic, eel-like salamanders that completely lack hind limbs, possessing only small forelimbs and permanent external gills
