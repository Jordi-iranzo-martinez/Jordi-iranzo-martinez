
Crustacea (from Latin *crustatus*: "having a shell, rind, or hard surface") 





### Crustacea taonomy

- **Ostracoda** (Seed Shrimp): Tiny, widespread crustaceans enclosed in a bivalve-like calcareous shell that look superficially like miniature clams.

- **Mystacocarida** (Mystacocarids): Microscopic, elongated crustaceans that live exclusively in the interstitial spaces between marine sand grains.

- **Ichthyostraca** (Fish Lice & Tongue Worms): A highly specialized clade of obligate parasites that includes _Arguloida_ (fish lice) and _Pentastomida_ (tongue worms).

- **Copepoda** (Copepods): Microscopic, teardrop-shaped crustaceans that represent one of the most abundant and ecologically vital components of marine zooplankton.

- **Thecostraca** (Barnacles & Allies): Sessile, filter-feeding crustaceans that cement themselves upside down to hard substrates, enclosing their bodies in calcareous plates.

- **Malacostraca** (Malacostracans): The largest and most economically significant class, containing over 60% of all crustacean species. _Examples: Crabs, lobsters, shrimps, woodlice, and krill._
