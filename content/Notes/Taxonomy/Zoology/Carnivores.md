
Carnivores



Carnivores taonomy

- Feliformia (cat-like)
	- **Felidae** (Cats): Hypercarnivorous predators with specialized retractile claws. _Examples: Lions, tigers, leopards, jaguars, cougars, and domestic cats._
	- **Hyaenidae** (Hyenas): Robust bone-cracking scavengers and hunters native to Africa and Asia. _Examples: Spotted hyenas, striped hyenas, and aardwolves._
	- **Herpestidae** (Mongooses): Small, agile predators renowned for their resistance to snake venom. _Examples: Meerkats and banded mongooses._
	- **Viverridae** (Civets & Genets): Primitive, small-to-medium-sized nocturnal carnivorans with cat-like appearances.
- Caniformia (dog-like)
	- **Canidae** (Dogs & Foxes): Highly social pack hunters or solitary omnivorous foragers. Examples: Gray wolves, domestic dogs, coyotes, jackals, and red foxes.
	- **Ursidae** (Bears): Large-bodied, heavy-set plantigrade mammals that are mostly omnivorous. Examples: Grizzly bears, polar bears, and giant pandas.
	- **Mustelidae** (Mustelids): Elongated, hyperactive predators with powerful scent glands. Examples: Otters, badgers, wolverines, ferrets, and weasels.
	- **Phocidae**: True seals / Earless seals (e.g., Harbor seals, elephant seals).
	- **Otariidae**: Eared seals (e.g., Sea lions and fur seals).
	- **Odobenidae**: Walruses (only one living species).

- **[Family Mustelidae](https://www.google.com/search?kgmid=%2Fm%2F04rqx&q=Family+Mustelidae) (Mustelids):** Elongated, hyperactive predators with powerful scent glands. _Examples: Otters, badgers, wolverines, ferrets, and weasels._
- **Pinnipedia (Clade of Marine Carnivorans):** Semiaquatic mammals whose limbs have evolved into flippers. It includes three core families:
    
    - **Family Phocidae:** True seals / Earless seals (e.g., Harbor seals, elephant seals).
    - **Family Otariidae:** Eared seals (e.g., Sea lions and fur seals).
    - **Family Odobenidae:** Walruses (only one living species).
