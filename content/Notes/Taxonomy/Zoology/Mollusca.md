
Mollusca (from Latin *mollis*: "soft")


### Mollusca taxonomy

- **Gastropoda**: snails and slugs
- **Bivalvia**: clams, oysters, mussels, and scallops
- **Cephalopoda**: octopuses, squids, cuttlefish, and nautiluses
- **Polyplacophora**: chitons
- **Scaphopoda**: tusk shells
- **Monoplacophora**: gloria maris
- **Aplacophora**: aplacophorans
