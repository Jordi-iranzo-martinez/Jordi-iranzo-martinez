[[Aguirre der Zorn Gottes]]


- **Spain:** Strong early-game advantage. They feature heavy armor infantry, mounted Conquistadors, and powerful crossbowmen.

- **England:** Specialists in long-range combat with highly disciplined musketeers and line infantry.

- **France:** Balanced economy and excellent fast-strike cavalry (mounted Chasseurs).

- **USA (The Thirteen Colonies):** Late-era faction focused on the Revolutionary War, featuring cheap militia and lethal sharpshooters.

- **Netherlands** _(Added in Fight Back)_: High defensive capabilities and strong commercial/trade bonuses.

- **Portugal** _(Added in Fight Back)_: Features exceptionally fast land and naval scouts.

- **Germany** _(Added in Fight Back)_: Represents mining expeditions and mercenary forces (like the Welser family operations).

- **Russia** _(Added in Fight Back)_: Represents the colonization of Alaska, featuring unique northern fur-trader units.

- **Aztecs:** Massive army sizes. They lack horses and gunpowder, but their Eagle and Jaguar Warriors are incredibly cheap, fast, and use poison or psychological fear mechanics.

- **Incas:** Similar to the Aztecs but focused on defensive stone architecture and rapid-fire slingers.

- **Mayas:** Guerrilla specialists who excel in forest ambushes and archers with poison arrows.

- **Sioux:** A highly unique nomadic faction. Their buildings are tipis that build instantly, and they possess the finest native cavalry in the game.

- **Hurons:** Highly resilient northern warriors who utilize the fog of war for ambush tactics.

- **Delawares:** Master hunters with an excellent vision range across the map.

- **Iroquois:** Versatile light infantry adapted to cold, rough terrain.

- **Pueblo:** Famous for their fortified mud-brick structures (_pueblos_) that act as heavily armed bunkers.

- **Haida** _(Added in Fight Back)_: Pacific Northwest tribe famous for being the only native faction with heavy **war canoes** to fight European ships.
