
The word "messiah" comes from the Hebrew _māšīaḥ_, which means **"the anointed one."** In ancient times, kings, priests, and prophets were anointed with sacred oils as a symbol that they had been chosen by God for a special divine mission.

- **In Judaism:** The Messiah is a future savior and king, a descendant of King David, who is expected to restore the nation of Israel, bring universal world peace, and unite humanity. Judaism is still awaiting his arrival.

- **In Christianity:** Christians believe that this Messiah has already come and is **Jesus of Nazareth**. In fact, the word **"Christ"** is the literal Greek translation (_Christos_) of the Hebrew word for Messiah.
