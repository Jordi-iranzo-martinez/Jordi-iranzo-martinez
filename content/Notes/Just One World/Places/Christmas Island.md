[Christmas Island](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Christmas+Island/@-10.4925153,105.6237201,24775m/data=!3m2!1e3!4b1!4m6!3m5!1s0x2ef59a27e3c0a7cf:0x15e7d6090475ea16!8m2!3d-10.447525!4d105.690449!16zL20vMDFwNWw?entry=ttu&g_ep=EgoyMDI2MTAyOC4wIKXMDSoASAFQAw%3D%3D) is a remote Australian external territory in the [[Indian Ocean]], located about 350 km (220 miles) south of Java, Indonesia, and world-famous for its annual mass migration of tens of millions of red crabs.

[[Australia]]

Every year at the start of the wet season (typically October–December), an estimated 50 to 100 million bright red land crabs (_Gecarcoidea natalis_) emerge from the rainforest and march simultaneously to the coast to mate and release their eggs into the ocean at high tide. To protect them, authorities close roads, set up plastic crab barriers, and build special overhead "crab bridges" across busy streets.

*English sea captain William Mynors named the island after sighting it on Christmas Day (December 25), 1643.*

Roughly 63% of the 135 km² island is protected as [Christmas Island National Park](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAESdgHEIkXXhI3lQpc6IEnJIQIuUFdSRmE-raqBUR1YtsaaW0eIy2jlJW-P41xf6wKPjC54YcBi_KLBK4eGOgtwkoOsciDJ7r07CyzyAnGK_P16hXk6V0LZz9PMLIr1HlHUmmjJiQB0A3YmcGR0HziIRLPoRJ64ftk). Alongside red crabs, it is home to robber (coconut) crabs (the world's largest terrestrial arthropod), rare nesting seabirds like Abbott's booby and the golden bosun, and surrounding coral reefs frequented by whale sharks between November and April.

*[[Coconut crab]]*

Its small population (around 1,700–2,000 residents) is a vibrant blend of Chinese, Malay, and European Australians, celebrating Lunar New Year, Hari Raya, and Christmas alike.
