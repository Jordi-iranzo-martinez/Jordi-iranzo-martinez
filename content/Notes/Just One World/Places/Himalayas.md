The [Himalayas](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Himalayas/@30.3858705,85.5921763,2782089m/data=!3m2!1e3!4b1!4m6!3m5!1s0x3995b9ebef1235bd:0x3ae1297b70640201!8m2!3d28.5983159!4d83.9310039!16zL20vMDlnbHc?entry=ttu&g_ep=EgoyMDI2MTAyOC4wIKXMDSoASAFQAw%3D%3D) (from Sanskrit *hima* meaning "snow" and *alaya* meaning "abode") are the highest mountain range on Earth, forming a massive 2,400-kilometer (1,500-mile) arc across [[Asia]] that separates the plains of the Indian subcontinent from the Tibetan Plateau.

*[[India]], [[High Mountains]]*

Spanning five countries —Nepal, [[India]], [[China]] (Tibet), Bhutan, and Pakistan— the range contains over 100 peaks exceeding 7,200 meters (23,600 feet), including Mount Everest, the world's tallest mountain above sea level at 8,848.86 meters (29,031.7 feet).

Because the Indian plate continues to push northward beneath Asia, the Himalayas remain geologically active—experiencing frequent earthquakes and rising by several millimeters every year.
