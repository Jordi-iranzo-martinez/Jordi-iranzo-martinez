木兰辞 (Ballad of Mulan) is a folk song surged during the Northern Wei Dynasty (386–534 CE) which tells the tory of 花木兰 (Hua Mulan) (meaning "Magnolia flower").

Hua Mulan was a young woman who disguises herself as a man to take her elderly father's place in the army.

Mulan is a mythological figure, not a real historical person
