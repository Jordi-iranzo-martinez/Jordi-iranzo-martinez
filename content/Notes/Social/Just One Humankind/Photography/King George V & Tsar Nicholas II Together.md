King George V of Great Britain and Tsar Nicholas II of Russia were first cousins: George V's mother (Princess Alexandra of Denmark) and Nicholas II's mother (Princess Dagmar of Denmark) were biological sisters.

Just one year later after the picture, the outbreak of World War I would pit their respective countries against each other, completely tearing their family structure apart
