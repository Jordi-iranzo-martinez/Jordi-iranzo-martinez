This iconic 1900 photograph shows soldiers representing the international military coalition of the Eight-Nation Alliance assembled during the Boxer Rebellion in Qing dynasty [[China]]

The soldiers are standing in line according to their respective empires and colonies. From left to right, the identification of the troops is

- **United Kingdom** (British Army artilleryman wearing a pith helmet)
- **United States** (U.S. Army cavalryman)
- **Australia** (Representing the British Empire colony)
- **British India** (Sikh trooper representing the British Indian Army)
- **German Empire** (Infantryman wearing a Pickelhaube helmet)
- **France** (French marine infantryman)
- **Austria-Hungary** (Austro-Hungarian navy sailor)
- **Kingdom of Italy** (Bersagliere infantryman with characteristic capercaillie feathers on his hat)
- **Empire of Japan** (Imperial Japanese Army infantryman)

*Although the Russian Empire was a prominent member of the alliance and deployed a large contingent of troops to northern China, their military forces are famously not represented in this specific lineup photograph*
