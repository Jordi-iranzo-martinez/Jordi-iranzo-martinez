
[Tasmania](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Tasmania,+Australia/@-41.4674859,146.160852,604166m/data=!3m2!1e3!4b1!4m6!3m5!1s0xaa7aed277e34facf:0x2a8fa5dd29404064!8m2!3d-42.0409059!4d146.8087323!16zL20vMDdjZng?entry=ttu&g_ep=EgoyMDI2MTAyOC4wIKXMDSoASAFQAw%3D%3D) is an isolated island state of [[Australia]], located approximately 240 kilometers (150 miles) south of the mainland, separated by the Bass Strait.

It is the country's largest island and stands as one of the most pristine and ecologically unique regions on Earth.

Nearly 42% of Tasmania's landmass is protected under national parks and ecological reserves, making up the Tasmanian Wilderness World Heritage Area (UNESCO).

- Tasmanian Devil (*Sarcophilus harrisii*) is the world's largest surviving carnivorous marsupial.
- Tasmanian Tiger (*Thylacinus cynocephalus*)
