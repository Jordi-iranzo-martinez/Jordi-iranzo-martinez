
Mesopotamia (from Greek land between rivers) i the region beteen the Tigris and Euphrates rivers.

It corresponds to modern-day Iraq, Kuwait, and parts of Syria and Turkey

nstead of being one single empire, this historical region was the home where multiple distinct civilizations rose and fell over thousands of years:

- **[[Sumerians]]:** the earliest known civilization in the region, famous for inventing the cuneiforme writing system you saw in the _Epic of Gilgamesh_.

- **[[Akkadians]]:** established the world's first multi-ethnic empire.

- **[[Babylonians]]:** known for King Hammurabi, his famous legal code, and the city of Babylon.

- **[[Assyrians]]:** a powerful military empire that dominated the region later on.
