On May 8, 2009, Carlton Philadelphia, a departing National Security Council staffer, brought his family to the Oval Office for a traditional farewell visit and photo session. Near the end of the meeting, his younger son, Jacob, timidly asked the President: _"Is your hair like mine?"_.

Instead of giving a simple verbal reply, President Obama leaned completely forward, lowered his head, and told the boy, _"Go ahead, touch it."_

. As Jacob reached out to feel the texture, Pete Souza snapped the photograph in a single frame. After touching it, Jacob remarked, _"Yeah, I think that's pretty much what I've got."_.
