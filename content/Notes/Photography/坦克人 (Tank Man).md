"Tank Man" is the moniker given to an unidentified individual who stood before a column of military tanks on Chang'an Avenue in Beijing on June 5, 1989. Taking place a day after the government crackdown in Tiananmen Square, this act of defiance became a global symbol of peaceful resistance.

The man stepped into the path of Type 59 tanks, repeatedly shifting position to block the lead vehicle and halting the convoy.
    
He climbed onto the tank's hull and conversed briefly with a crew member before climbing down.

When the tanks resumed movement, he blocked them again until bystanders pulled him away into the crowd.
