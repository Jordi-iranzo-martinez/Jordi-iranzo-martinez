As some pokémons are inspired in concret species, other ones are based on groups of them.

Taxonomy is the science that classifies biodiversity.

For doing so, it has a hierarchical structure, with each type comprising the other ones. From more to less, they are the followings: kingdom, phylum, class, order, family, genus, and specie.

- **Kingdom**: animals, plants, fungus
- **Philum**: vertebrates, 
- **Class**: sea stars, insects, arachnids, mammals, birds, reptiles, amphibians, cartilaginous fishes, and ray-finned fishes.
- **Order**: rays, lionants, centipeds, 
- **Family**: felids (cats), canids (dogs), pangolins, chamaelions, poison dart frogs, pistol shrimp, pitcher plants, 

Remoras are marine fish known for their fascinating ability to attach themselves to larger animals like sharks, turtles, rays, and even boats. They do this using a special suction disc located on the top of their heads.

Rays (order Rajiformes) are cartilaginous fish related to sharks, belonging to the order  or, in modern classifications, the superorder Batoidea. They have a flattened body with broad pectoral fins fused to the head, giving them a “wing-like” shape.
