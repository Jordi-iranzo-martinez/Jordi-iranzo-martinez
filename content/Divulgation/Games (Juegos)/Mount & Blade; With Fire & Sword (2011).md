Mount & Blade; With Fire & Sword (2011) is set in 1655 in Europe, based on the historical fiction novel _With Fire and Sword_ by Polish author Henryk Sienkiewicz.

### Factions
#### The Kingdom of Sweden

The Kingdom of Sweden was known as the "Stormaktstiden" (The Age of Greatness). During this time, Sweden transformed from a poor, isolated northern kingdom into a dominant European superpower.

Sweden entered the Thirty Years' War (1618–1648), marching deep into Germany. Although Gustavus Adolphus was killed in battle in 1632, his brilliant generals secured massive territorial gains for Sweden by the end of the war in the Peace of Westphalia (1648).

The game begins around 1655, just as King Carl X Gustaf (the ruler you meet in the game) takes the throne. Sweden controlled almost the entire Baltic coast, turning the Baltic Sea into a "Swedish Lake" (*Dominium maris baltici*).

Looking to expand further and secure wealth for his massive standing army, Carl X Gustaf launched a massive, unprovoked invasion of the Polish-Lithuanian Commonwealth in 1655. This brutal historical event is known in Polish history as "The Deluge" (Potop).

Swedish armies captured Warsaw and Kraków with terrifying speed. They utilized superior gunpowder artillery and highly drilled infantry to overwhelm the traditional Polish forces.

While Carl X Gustaf achieved great military victories, Sweden overextended itself. Surrounded by bitter rivals (Russia, Poland, and Denmark), the empire was constantly at war.

The Swedish Empire finally collapsed a few decades later during the Great Northern War (1700–1721). King Charles XII (Carl XII) fought brilliantly but was ultimately defeated by Peter the Great of Russia at the Battle of Poltava in 1709. This defeat marked the end of Sweden as a superpower and the rise of the Russian Empire.

#### The Polish-Lithuanian Commonwealth


The Polish-Lithuanian Commonwealth) is one of the most iconic, wealthy, and culturally distinct factions.

During the mid-17th century, Poland finds itself in a state of existential crisis, surrounded by enemies on all sides—a period historically referred to as "The Deluge".

It boasts an elite military tradition that relies heavily on shock cavalry rather than the gunpowder-and-pike formations favored by Sweden.

Polish Winged Hussars

Pancerni were medium-to-heavy cavalry units. They are cheaper and quicker to recruit than Hussars but still highly effective at flanking and cutting down light infantry.

Their infantry is weak. 

Once the largest and most tolerant superpower in Europe, a series of catastrophic events struck all at once:

1. **The Khmelnytsky Uprising (1648):** A massive rebellion by the Ukrainian Cossacks shattered Polish domestic stability and stripped them of vital border territories.

2. **The Deluge (1655):** Seizing on Poland's weakness, King Carl X of Sweden invaded from the north, while Russia invaded from the east. Polish forces were overwhelmed by modern Swedish gunpowder tactics, leading to the sacking of Warsaw and Kraków.

3. **The Winged Hussars' Sunset:** While the Hussars famously saved Europe decades later at the **Battle of Vienna (1683)**, the Commonwealth never truly recovered from the economic devastation of this era, eventually leading to its political decline.

#### The Tsardom of Russia

The Tsardom of Russia was ruled by Tsar Alexei I Mikhailovich, the second monarch of the Romanov dynasty. 

The Russian military offers a versatile, heavily armored roster that acts as a middle ground between Western European gunpowder tactics and Eastern European melee prowess:


[[Стрельцы (Streltsy) (2024)]]

The Streltsy (Marksmen): Russia possesses an incredibly unique ranged roster, including New Order Marksmen. Almost all Russian shooters are equipped with a Bardiche (a massive, two-handed poleaxe) alongside their firearms. This means that unlike Swedish or Polish musketeers—who are defenseless in close combat—Russian marksmen can chop down enemy cavalry and infantry with ease if they get breached.

- **Spearmen & Infantry:** Russian melee infantry utilizes a balance of medium-length spears and swords, making them sturdy defenders during castle sieges. 

- **The Noble Guard:** The top-tier Russian cavalry is heavily armored and highly versatile, capable of locking down flanks, though they lack the overwhelming lance-charge power of Poland's Winged Hussars.

#### The Zaporozhian Host

The Zaporozhian Host represent a fiercely independent, democratic society of warrior-frontiersmen known as cossacks ([[Cossacks]]).

Located in the southern steppes (modern-day Ukraine), they are a highly unique faction that relies on mobile warfare, unconventional tactics, and a fierce hatred of Polish feudal rule.

The Cossacks are led by a Hetman—a military commander elected by the Cossack council. 

Hetman Bohdan Khmelnytsky launched a massive, bloody rebellion in 1648 against the Polish Commonwealth, completely reshaping Eastern European history.

Cossack infantry consists of highly skilled musketeers. While they lack the defense of Russia's Streltsy or the discipline of Sweden's Lifeguards, they have exceptionally fast reload speeds and high damage output.

While not fully controllable in real-time battles like a vehicle, the Cossacks are famous for their defensive wagon trains. In tactical battles, their infantry excels at firing from behind cover.

Units like the Cossack Captain or Cossack Horse Marksmen carry pistols, muskets, or sabers. They cannot clash head-on with Polish Winged Hussars, but they are unmatched at circling the enemy, baiting charges, and shooting down troops from horseback.


#### The Crimean Khanate

The Crimean Khanate 

Located in the southernmost region of the map (the Crimean peninsula and the surrounding steppes), this faction represents the Turco-Mongol state that historically operated as a vassal of the Ottoman Empire.

The faction is ruled by Khan Islam III Giray. In the game, he is caught in a complex geopolitical web. Historically, the Khanate formed a fragile, opportunistic alliance with Bohdan Khmelnytsky's Cossacks to weaken the Polish Commonwealth, but the Khan is always looking out for his own territorial expansion and the capture of Christian captives for the slave trade.

They do not have muskets, pistols, or grenades. Instead, they rely on unrivaled speed, agility, and archery:


- **Crimean Khanate Horse Archers:** These are the signature units of the faction. They are incredibly fast and possess elite archery skills. In open-field battles, they can circle enemy infantry formations indefinitely, raining down arrows and whittling down opponents without ever getting hit.

- **Mirza and Seymen Guards:** Their heavy cavalry units swap firearms for traditional lances, heavy composite bows, and deadly scimitars. They excel at hunting down isolated Swedish or Russian musketeers.

- **Fragile Infantry:** Khanate infantry is very weak, poorly armored, and lacks firearms. Consequently, this faction struggles heavily during castle sieges, where their horses are taken away and they are forced into tight, face-to-face infantry grinds against heavily armored opponents.


### Aftermatch

In 1655 was the peak of Swedish and Polish power. Russia was seen as a isolated, backward state. However, after decades of warfare

However, under Tsar Alexei and later his son, Peter the Great, Russia modernized its military. By the end of the Great Northern War (1700–1721), Russia completely defeated the Swedish Empire at the _Battle of Poltava (1709)_. Then seized the Baltic coast, built the new capital of St. Petersburg, and officially declared itself the Russian Empire, becoming the dominant superpower of Eastern Europe for the next two centuries.

Despite surviving "The Deluge" and later saving Central Europe from the Ottomans at the Battle of Vienna (1683), Poland was economically ruined and politically broken. Over the next century, its neighbors (mainly Russia and Prussia) slowly annexed its territories until Poland disappeared from the map in 1795.

To escape Polish rule, Hetman Bohdan Khmelnytsky signed a treaty aligning the Cossacks with Russia. Over time, the Russian Tsars stripped the Cossacks of their democratic independence, eventually dissolving the Zaporozhian Host and absorbing Ukraine into the Russian Empire.

Sweden's empire lacked the population and resources to defend an empire surrounded by enemies. Following their total defeat by Russia in 1721, Sweden lost all its territories in Germany, the Baltic states, and later Finland. Sweden was forced to abandon its imperial ambitions and transitioned into the peaceful, neutral Scandinavian nation we know today.

The Khanate continued its hit-and-run raids for several decades, but as modern gunpowder, artillery, and professional standing armies advanced, traditional horse archers became obsolete. With their Ottoman protectors weakening, the Crimean Khanate was invaded and annexed by Russia in 1783 under Catherine the Great.
