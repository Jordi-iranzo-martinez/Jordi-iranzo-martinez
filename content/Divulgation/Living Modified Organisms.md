### The Supplementary Protocol on 

The Nagoya-Kuala Lumpur Supplementary Protocol

  

The Nagoya-Kuala Lumpur Supplementary Protocol (read the full text) amplifies the previous one. It refers to the liability and redress about the damages on transboundary movements. Intentional, unintentional, and illegal.

  

These damages are adverse effects measurable or observable. Also the significant ones, according to its duration, extension, and impact on the ecological services and human health.

  

Parties have to create legislative measures to manage it in their territory. Even if the responsibles are non-Parties. And also to establish a causal link between the damage an the organism.

  

Parties shall have operators to manage the damage. They depend on the competent authority. If a damage happens, the operator informs it. Both evaluate the damage, and the competent authority determines which response measures the operator should take.

  

They are actions to manage prevent or minimize the damage and restore the biological diversity. To the previous state, or, if the competent authority determinates it is not possible, on another place.

  

Living modified organisms for direct use as food or feed, or for processing, for contained use, and for intentional introduction into the environment.
