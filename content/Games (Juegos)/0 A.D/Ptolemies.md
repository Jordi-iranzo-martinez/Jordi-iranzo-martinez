Ptolomeo I Sóter (367 a.C. – 282 a.C.) was one of the guardaespaldas of Alejandro Magno.

After the death of Alejandro Magno in Babilonia, while was being carried back to Macedonia, Ptolomeo stole its body and brought it to Alejandría (Egypt).

He was named Pharaon and ordered to built the Great Library of Alexandry and started the building of the Alejandría Lighthouse.

To make that the native Egyptians and Greeks co-lived, hired theologists and invented a new God called Serapis, with the appearence of a bearded Green God (such as Zeus) but the mystic functions of Egyptians Gods.
