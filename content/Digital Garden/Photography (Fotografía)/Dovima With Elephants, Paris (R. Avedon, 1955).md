Dovima was the highest-paid and most iconic supermodel of the 1950s. The brilliance of the image relies entirely on its extreme visual juxtapositions:

at the Cirque d'Hiver (Winter Circus) in Paris

Before Richard Avedon, fashion photography was rigid, clinical, and studio-bound. Models stood like lifeless, frozen mannequins inside controlled environments. Avedon completely shattered this tradition by bringing high fashion out into the real world and injecting it with movement, emotion, and drama.
