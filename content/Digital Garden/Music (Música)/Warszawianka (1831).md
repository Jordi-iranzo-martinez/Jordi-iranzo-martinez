
"Warszawianka" (translated as The Varsovian or The Song of Warsaw) refers to two highly influential Polish patriotic and revolutionary anthems.

- Written to support the **November Uprising (1830–1831)**, a major armed rebellion by Polish nationalists against the rule of the Russian Empire.

- **Origin:** The original lyrics were actually written in French by the poet Casimir Delavigne. It was later translated into Polish by Karol Sienkiewicz and set to music by Karol Kurpiński.

- **Legacy:** It has a strong nationalistic and martial tone. Today, it remains highly revered and serves as the **official march of the Polish Armed Forces**.
