Ryan Hrljac is 

Ryan Hreljac (born May 31, 1991, in Ottawa, Canada) is a celebrated Canadian activist and humanitarian. He became globally famous as a child for his extraordinary determination to tackle the global water crisis, which led to the creation of the Ryan's Well Foundation.


In 1998, when Ryan was just six years old, his first-grade teacher explained to the class that many children in developing countries—particularly in Africa—had no access to clean water. She mentioned that kids had to walk for miles to fetch dirty water that made them sick, and added that a small well could be built for about **$70**.

- **The Chore Plan:** Shocked to learn that life wasn't equally fair for everyone, Ryan went home and begged his mother for $70. His parents told him he had to earn it himself. For months, he did extra household chores like vacuuming, washing windows, and clearing brush to save every penny.

- **The Real Cost:** When he finally proudly brought his $70 to a water charity, they gently explained reality to him: $70 might buy a small hand pump, but drilling a real, sustainable well actually cost **$2,000**.

🚀 The Movement Grows

Instead of giving up, 7-year-old Ryan doubled down. He gave speeches to local community groups, service clubs like the Rotary Club, and schools to raise awareness. His pure, naive conviction touched his neighbors and eventually caught the attention of the media. By 1999, he had raised the full $2,000. His very first well was drilled next to **Angolo Primary School in northern Uganda**. When Ryan visited the school a year later at age 9, he was greeted by hundreds of children singing his name in celebration. There, he met his pen pal, **Jimmy Akana**, a Ugandan orphan who later became an official part of the Hreljac family when Ryan's parents legally adopted him years later. 🌍 Ryan's Well Foundation Today

To manage the influx of donations pouring in from inspired people worldwide, the Ryan's Well Foundation was officially established in 2001. [[1](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAESfwHrOzAVqHcj-PIlQZeeaX3Lz8u3c-tQrt54YRAmrO7wqUcZluoz0KvRp-EkklGTqKwWI2AETSaWA_SgAHY1CNJANadrA2Caxk3BxKsl-YARCEbM3T-NlA2r3EFvhBa_YDgc4IL1hJUrzOSpfUwQOyzqmqtJV4bkZxM9TbpYwt8)]

Over the decades, Ryan's childhood dream has transformed into a highly professional NGO. The foundation has built **more than 1,700 water projects and over 1,200 latrines across 30 developing nations**. His work has brought clean water, improved sanitation, and dynamic community health education to nearly **1 million people**.
