The Covenant of Mayors for Climate & Energy is the largest global movement of local governments voluntarily committed to climate and energy action.

Launched in 2008 by the European Commission ([[European Union]]), the initiative empowers towns, cities, and regions to meet and exceed official European Union and global climate targets through localized strategies.

Signatory cities align their local policies under a shared vision of achieving climate neutrality by 2050. They focus on three main areas:

- **Mitigation:** Accelerating the reduction of greenhouse gas emissions, aiming for at least a 55% reduction by 2030.

- **Adaptation:** Increasing urban resilience to prepare for and withstand the unavoidable impacts of climate change (such as floods, droughts, and heatwaves).

- **Energy Poverty:** Alleviating financial vulnerability by ensuring secure, clean, and affordable energy access for all citizens.


What started as a European initiative has expanded into a massive, worldwide coalition:

The **[EU Covenant of Mayors](https://eu-mayors.ec.europa.eu/en/home)** manages the European branch, connecting thousands of local authorities representing hundreds of millions of citizens.

The **[Global Covenant of Mayors](https://www.globalcovenantofmayors.org/) ** (GCoM) was formed by merging the European movement with the international _Compact of Mayors_. It now units **over 14,000 cities across 150 countries**.

Technical and scientific assistance is provided by the European Commission’s Joint Research Centre. The movement continues to shape local climate policy through major international gatherings:
