The [United Nations Convention to Combat Desertification in Those Countries Experiencing Serious Drought and/or Desertification](https://www.unccd.int), Particularly in Africa 

Adopted in **1994** and entering into force in **1996**, it is one of the three **"Rio Conventions"** alongside the UNFCCC (Climate Change) and the CBD (Biodiversity). It currently has **197 signatory parties**, including the European Union.


The convention focuses specifically on **drylands**—arid, semi-arid, and dry sub-humid areas—where some of the world's most vulnerable ecosystems and populations live. Its core principles revolve around:

- **Combating Land Degradation:** Promoting long-term strategies to maintain and restore soil productivity, rehabilitate partly degraded land, and reclaim desertified territory.

- **Drought Mitigation:** Establishing collaborative early-warning systems and framework protocols to help countries build structural resilience against acute, climate-driven drought cycles.

- **The "Bottom-Up" Approach:** Unlike other top-down international mechanisms, the UNCCD legally stresses the active participation of **local communities, farmers, and indigenous populations** in designing and implementing land-management roadmaps.

**Modern Strategic Context & Institutional Framework**

The convention works dynamically beyond its original 1990s framework, focusing heavily on financing and global ecological restoration targets:

- **The Great Green Wall (GGW):** The UNCCD serves as a primary international body coordinating and accelerating Africa's flagship _Great Green Wall_ initiative. The current strategy focuses on transforming financial commitments into verified socio-economic results, local job creation, and land-positive agricultural systems across dryland corridors.

- **The COP17 Summit Outcomes:** Member states gathered for the historic **COP17 summit in Ulaanbaatar, Mongolia**. Operating under the core theme **"Restoring Land, Restoring Hope,"** the conference marked a pivotal shift toward human-centric solutions. COP17 advanced **US$1.3 billion for targeted land restoration** with a particular focus on protecting fragile rangelands.

- **The Global Drought Resilience Instrument (DRIF):** COP17 saw the official launch of the [Drought Resilience Investment F](https://www.unccd.int/sites/default/files/2025-09/DRIF%20press%20release%20EN.pdf)acility (DRIF), the very first dedicated global financial mechanism engineered exclusively to fund rapid response infrastructure and long-term land stability measures in drought-prone nations.

- **Synergistic Clean Energy Frameworks:** Current technical directives heavily emphasize dual-use land systems. These systems systematically pair renewable energy installations with sustainable grazing and community-led water management to protect local soils while providing low-cost electricity across the Global South.
