**Favio Chávez & The Cateura Recycled Orchestra**
**From waste to music**

[The Recycled Orchestra of Cateura](https://www.recycledorchestracateura.com/) is an educational project by music teacher Favio Chávez  in Cateura (Paraguay) on upcycling waste material into music instruments for poor people. 





The documentary film [*Landfill Harmoniv*](http://landfillharmonicmovie.com) (2015), by Brad Allgood and Graham Townsley.

[Sonidos de la Tierra](https://sonidosdelatierra.org.py/) ("World Sounds" in Spanish), to export the project in order to defend teenagers and young people from extreme poverty in Paraguay.
