**The founder of 'The Ghana Bamboo Bikes Initiative'**

Bernice Dapaah is a Ghanaian social entrepreneur who is the founder and chief executive officer of the Ghana Bamboo Bike Initiative in Kumasi.

<iframe title="vimeo-player" src="https://player.vimeo.com/video/88258932?h=8d6686f14d" width="640" height="360" frameborder="0"    allowfullscreen></iframe>


The [Ghana Bamboo Bikes Initiative](https://ghanabamboobikes.org/)


She is member of [World Economic Forum](https://www.weforum.org/people/bernice-dapaah/), was also [interviewed in WIPO Green](https://unfccc.int/climate-action/momentum-for-change/women-for-results/ghana-bamboo-bikes-initiative), the Market place for Sustainable Technology. 

She was also cited as an example of [Climate Action in the UNFCCC](https://unfccc.int/climate-action/momentum-for-change/women-for-results/ghana-bamboo-bikes-initiative),
