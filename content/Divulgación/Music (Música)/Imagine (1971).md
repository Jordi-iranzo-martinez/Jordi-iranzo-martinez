gImagine" by John Lennon was released on **October 11, 1971** in the US (and October 8, 1971 in the UK), as a single and as the title track of the album "Imagine."

A few additional details:

- It was written by John Lennon, with uncredited contribution from **Yoko Ono** — she was officially recognized as co-writer decades later, in 2017.
- The album was recorded mostly at Lennon's home studio at **Tittenhurst Park**, England.
- It has become one of the most iconic songs of the 20th century, with a pacifist message imagining a world without borders, religion, or material possessions.
- Interestingly, it wasn't an enormous commercial hit right away — its status as a cultural anthem grew over time, and it's now considered one of the greatest songs ever written.
