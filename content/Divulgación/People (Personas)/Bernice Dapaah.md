Bernice Dapaah is a renowned Ghanaian social entrepreneur and the founder and CEO of the **Ghana Bamboo Bikes Initiative**. She is globally celebrated for transforming an abundant natural resource—**bamboo—into eco-friendly bicycles**, turning this invention into a powerful tool for sustainable mobility, job creation, and social inclusion in West Africa.

Her journey and the impact of her project are centered around these key areas:

🚲 Bicycles for Education and Sustainable Mobility

Bernice was inspired by her own childhood in rural Ghana, where she lived with her grandfather (a forester) and had to walk over three hours a day just to attend school.

- As an adult, she realized that long walking distances under the scorching sun forced thousands of children in isolated villages to drop out of school.
- In 2009, after graduating with a degree in Business Administration, she decided to build an enterprise that manufactured bicycle frames using local bamboo. Bamboo proved to be an ideal material: it is lightweight, highly shock-absorbent, durable, and naturally captures large amounts of CO₂ while growing.
- The Buy-One-Give-One Model: For every bicycle sold to international markets (such as Europe or the United States), the initiative donates a bicycle to a student or teacher in rural Ghana, dramatically cutting down their daily commute.

👩‍🏭 Female Empowerment and Local Employment

Based in the city of Kumasi, her factory operates under a circular economy and fair trade model. The workshop prioritizes **hiring and training women and unemployed young people**, equipping them with mechanical skills, economic independence, and stable livelihoods in a region with high unemployment rates.

🌱 Environmental Commitment and Reforestation

To ensure the business remains truly sustainable and to combat deforestation, Bernice implemented a strict ecological policy: **for every single bamboo stem harvested to build a bike, her team plants ten new ones**.

🌍 International Recognition

For her leadership in clean mobility and social innovation, Bernice Dapaah has received numerous accolades from the **United Nations** (including awards from UNEP) and was named a _Young Global Leader_ by the **World Economic Forum**. She also serves on the advisory board of the global climate platform WIPO GREEN in Switzerland.
