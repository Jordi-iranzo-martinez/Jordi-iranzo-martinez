**🌡️ Warm, humid climate:**

- **No polar ice caps** = more water available globally
- **Natural greenhouse effect** (high CO₂ levels)
- **Global temperatures** 6-8°C warmer than today
- **Year-round growing seasons** even at high latitudes

**🌱 Explosion of primary producers:**

- **Dense forests** of conifers, ferns, and cycads
- **First flowering plants** = increased plant diversity
- **Higher oxygen levels** in atmosphere (up to 35% vs 21% today)
- **Massive plant biomass** = abundant food sources

**🦕 Advantages of gigantism:**

- **Thermoregulation** = easier to maintain body temperature (large bodies lose heat more slowly)
- **Predator avoidance** = fewer predators can attack giants
- **Resource access** = reach high tree canopies that smaller animals can't
- **Metabolic efficiency** = lower relative energy costs per kilogram of body weight
- **Digestive advantages** = longer gut retention time for processing tough plant material

**🌍 Geological factors:**

- **Shallow seas** covered much of the continents = more coastal habitats
- **Stable continental positions** = consistent climate patterns
- **Abundant mineral nutrients** from volcanic activity

**🔄 Evolutionary arms race:**

- **Predator-prey escalation** = bigger herbivores needed bigger predators
- **Competition for resources** = size advantage in reaching food
- **Sexual selection** = larger individuals had mating advantages

# Dinosaurs



**The Apatosaurus** (_Apatosaurus louisae_) was a massive long-necked sauropod dinosaur that lived during the Late Jurassic period. It could reach lengths of up to 23 meters and weighed around 16-22 tons, feeding on ferns, conifers, and other vegetation.

**The Triceratops** (_Triceratops horridus_) was a large herbivorous ceratopsian dinosaur known for its distinctive three facial horns and large bony frill. It lived during the Late Cretaceous period and could weigh up to 12 tons.

**The Saurolophus** (_Saurolophus osborni_) was a duck-billed hadrosaur dinosaur that lived during the Late Cretaceous period. It was characterized by its distinctive backward-pointing cranial crest and could reach lengths of up to 12 meters.

**The Stegosaurus** (_Stegosaurus stenops_) was an armored herbivorous dinosaur from the Late Jurassic period, famous for its distinctive double row of plates along its back and four sharp spikes on its tail called a "thagomizer."

**The Tyrannosaurus** (_Tyrannosaurus rex_) was one of the largest land predators ever known, living during the Late Cretaceous period. It could reach lengths of 12-13 meters and had powerful jaws with teeth up to 20 cm long.

**The Deinonychus** (_Deinonychus antirrhopus_) was a medium-sized raptor dinosaur from the Early Cretaceous period, known for its large sickle-shaped claw on each foot and its likely pack-hunting behavior.

# Non-dinosaurs

**The Pteranodon** (_Pteranodon longiceps_) was a flying reptile (pterosaur), not a dinosaur. It lived during the Late Cretaceous period and had a wingspan of up to 7 meters, making it one of the largest flying animals ever.

**The Triadobatrachus** (_Triadobatrachus massinoti_) was an early amphibian, not a dinosaur. It lived during the Early Triassic period (about 250 million years ago) and is considered a transitional form between frogs and their ancestors.
