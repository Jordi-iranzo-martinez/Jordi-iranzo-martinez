

In biology, there is no single sentence that defines life perfectly. Instead, science defines life by **what it does**. To be considered a living organism (from a microscopic bacterium to a giant whale), an entity must fulfill these conditions:

- **Organization & Cells:** It must have a structured blueprint (DNA/RNA) contained within cells.

- **Metabolism:** It must extract energy from its environment (like a plant absorbing sunlight or a human eating food) to stay organized. Life is a constant battle against **entropy** (chaos and decay).

- **Homeostasis:** The ability to maintain internal stability (like sweating to cool down when it's hot).

- **Response to Stimuli:** Reacting to the environment (a plant bending toward light, or an animal running away from danger).

- **Growth, Reproduction & Evolution:** The capacity to grow, replicate its genetic code, and adapt over generations through natural selection.
