Bernice Dapaah is a renowned Ghanaian social entrepreneur and the founder and CEO of the [Ghana Bamboo Bikes Initiative](https://ghanabamboobikes.org/).

She is globally celebrated for transforming an abundant natural resource—bamboo—into eco-friendly bicycles, turning this invention into a powerful tool for sustainable mobility, job creation, and social inclusion in West Africa.

She is member of [World Economic Forum](https://www.weforum.org/people/bernice-dapaah/), was also [interviewed in WIPO Green](https://unfccc.int/climate-action/momentum-for-change/women-for-results/ghana-bamboo-bikes-initiative), the Market place for Sustainable Technology.
