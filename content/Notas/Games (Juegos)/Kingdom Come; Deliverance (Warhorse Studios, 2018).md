
Kingdom Come; Deliverance (Warhorse Studios, 2018) is set in 1403 in the Kingdom of Bohemia (modern-day Czech Republic), a region of the Holy Roman Empire, during a brutal civil war following the death of Emperor Charles IV.

After the death of Emperor Charles IV, Wenceslao IV heritages the crown of the Kingdom of Bohemia. However, he is a weak governor, lazy and uncapable, named "The Indolent".

Profiting the unhappiness of nobility, his half-brother the King Segismund of Hungary kidnaps Wenceslao and movilizes an army of cuman mercenaries to invade Bohemia and sack his silver mines.

### The Cumans

The Cumans (also known as "the Polovtsians") were a nomadic Turkic people who originally came from the steppes of Central Asia and Southern Russia. They were horse archers, famous for their speed, brutality, and skills at full gallop.

In the 13th century, the Mongol Empire invaded their homeland, so many of them migrated west to Hungary. The Hungarian kings allowed them to settle in exchange for their military service as elite shock troops.

Cumans wear distinctive eastern-style scale mail, pointed helmets, and metal faceplates or masks forged in the shape of human faces to protect them from arrows and inspire fear in their enemies.
