The "monster" was actually a cheap toy submarine bought from a local Woolworths store.

Spurling meticulously sculpted the neck and head using plastic wood.

Wetherell took the photo very close to the water's surface to make the tiny toy look massive, using the natural ripples of the lake to mimic large ocean waves.
