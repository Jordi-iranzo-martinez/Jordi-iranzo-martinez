
### VIRUSES (Highlighted Species):

- **Bacteriophage T4** - Looks like a lunar lander with mechanical legs and a syringe tail
- **Tobacco Mosaic Virus** - Crystalline structure that forms perfect geometric patterns
- **Mimivirus** - Giant virus larger than some bacteria, challenges what we consider "alive"
- **Ebola virus** - Snake-like filamentous structure that coils and uncoils

### BACTERIA (Highlighted Species):

- **Deinococcus radiodurans** - "Conan the Bacterium" - survives 1,000x lethal radiation doses
- **Magnetospirillum magneticum** - Contains magnetic crystals for navigation
- **Thiobacillus ferrooxidans** - Literally eats rocks and metals for energy
- **Pyrococcus furiosus** - Lives at boiling temperatures (100°C+)
- **Vibrio fischeri** - Bioluminescent, creates living light
- **Bacillus subtilis** - Forms indestructible spores that can survive space

### ARCHAEA (Highlighted Species):

- **Methanococcus jannaschii** - Eats CO₂ and produces methane
- **Halobacterium salinarum** - Lives in salt concentrations that would kill everything else
- **Pyrodictium occultum** - Thrives at 110°C in underwater volcanic vents
- **Nanoarchaeum equitans** - Smallest known cellular organism, lives as parasite
