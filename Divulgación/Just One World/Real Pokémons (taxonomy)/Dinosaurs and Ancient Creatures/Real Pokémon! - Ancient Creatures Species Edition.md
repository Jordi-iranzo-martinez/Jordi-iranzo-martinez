**Precambrian Era (4.6 billion - 541 million years ago):**

- **Early life forms** (bacteria, archaea, early eukaryotes)

**Paleozoic Era (541 - 252 million years ago):**

_Cambrian Period (541 - 485 mya):_

- **Anomalocaridids** (Anomalocaris - early ocean predators)
- **Trilobites** (marine arthropods)
- **Early echinoderms** (sea lilies/crinoids)

_Ordovician Period (485 - 444 mya):_

- **Cephalopods** (early nautiloids)
- **Brachiopods** (marine filter feeders)

_Silurian Period (444 - 419 mya):_

- **Early fish** (jawless fish)
- **Eurypterids** (sea scorpions)

_Devonian Period (419 - 359 mya):_

- **Placodermi** (armored fish)
- **Early tetrapods** (first land vertebrates)
- **Coelacanths** (lobe-finned fish, "living fossils")

_Carboniferous Period (359 - 299 mya):_

- **Giant arthropods** (giant dragonflies, millipedes)
- **Early reptiles**

_Permian Period (299 - 252 mya):_

- **Synapsids** (early mammal ancestors)
- **Ammonoids** (ammonites - spiral-shelled cephalopods)

**Mesozoic Era (252 - 66 million years ago) - "Age of Reptiles":**

_Triassic Period (252 - 201 mya):_

- **Early dinosaurs**
- **Pterosaurs** (flying reptiles)
- **Marine reptiles** (ichthyosaurs, plesiosaurs)

_Jurassic Period (201 - 145 mya):_

- **Sauropods** (long-necked dinosaurs)
- **Theropods** (carnivorous dinosaurs)
- **Stegosaurs** (plated dinosaurs)

_Cretaceous Period (145 - 66 mya):_

- **Tyrannosaurus rex** (apex predator theropod)
- **Triceratops** (three-horned ceratopsian)
- **Pachycephalosaurus** (thick-skulled dinosaur)
- **Pterodactyls** (pterosaurs)
- **Archaeopteryx** (transitional bird-dinosaur)

**Cenozoic Era (66 million years ago - present) - "Age of Mammals":**

_Paleogene Period (66 - 23 mya):_

- **Early mammals** (rise of mammalian diversity)
- **Early primates**

_Neogene Period (23 - 2.6 mya):_

- **Megafauna** (giant mammals)

_Quaternary Period (2.6 mya - present):_

- **Ice Age megafauna** (mammoths, saber-toothed cats)
- **Early humans**
