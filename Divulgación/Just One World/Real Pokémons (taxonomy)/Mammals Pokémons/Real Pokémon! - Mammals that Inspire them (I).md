# Marsupials

Marsupials are mammals whose young are born immature and develop in a pouch (marsupium). Many live in Oceania because they face less competition with placental mammals there.

It comprises three main orders: **Diprotodontia** (kangaroos and koalas), **Dasyuromorphia** (Tasmanian devils), and **Peramelemorphia** (bandicoots).

**Kangaroos** (_Macropodidae_) are large, with powerful hind legs for hopping and strong arms for boxing. Their hind legs act like springs, allowing them to leap long distances (over 9 meters in a single bound) and reach speeds up to 56 km/h (35 mph). They are herbivores with specialized stomachs for fermenting plant material.

**The Koala** (_Phascolarctos cinereus_) is a marsupial native to Australia with a stout, tailless body, large fluffy ears, and a diet consisting mainly of eucalyptus leaves, which are toxic to most other animals.

The koala (_Phascolarctos cinereus_) is a marsupial native to Australia, known for its stout, tailless body, large head with fluffy ears, and its diet consisting mainly of eucalyptus leaves.

**The Tasmanian devils** (_Sarcophilus harrisii_) are aggressive and carnivorous with powerful jaws. They feed mainly on carrion and small animals, using their strong bite to crush bones.

**Bandicoots** are small and omnivorous, with pointed snouts. They're skilled diggers, using their pointed snouts to forage for food underground.
