#### **PHYLUM: ASCOMYCOTA** (Sac Fungi)

- **Class: Saccharomycetes**
    - Order: Saccharomycetales (yeasts like _Saccharomyces cerevisiae_)
- **Class: Pezizomycetes**
    - Order: Pezizales (cup fungi, truffles)
- **Class: Sordariomycetes**
    - Order: Hypocreales (Cordyceps, _Trichoderma_)
    - Order: Sordariales (dung fungi)
- **Class: Leotiomycetes**
    - Order: Helotiales (many plant pathogens)
- **Class: Eurotiomycetes**
    - Order: Eurotiales (_Penicillium_, _Aspergillus_)

#### **PHYLUM: BASIDIOMYCOTA** (Club Fungi)

- **Class: Agaricomycetes**
    - Order: Agaricales (mushrooms, toadstools)
    - Order: Boletales (boletes, porcini)
    - Order: Polyporales (bracket fungi, polypores)
    - Order: Russulales (_Russula_, _Lactarius_)
    - Order: Cantharellales (chanterelles)
    - Order: Phallales (stinkhorns)
    - Order: Thelephorales (tooth fungi)
- **Class: Tremellomycetes**
    - Order: Tremellales (jelly fungi)
- **Class: Dacrymycetes**
    - Order: Dacrymycetales (jelly fungi)
- **Class: Ustilaginomycetes**
    - Order: Ustilaginales (smut fungi)
- **Class: Pucciniomycetes**
    - Order: Pucciniales (rust fungi)

#### **PHYLUM: CHYTRIDIOMYCOTA** (Chytrids)

- **Class: Chytridiomycetes**
    - Order: Chytridiales (aquatic fungi)

#### **PHYLUM: ZYGOMYCOTA** (Conjugated Fungi)

- **Class: Zygomycetes**
    - Order: Mucorales (bread molds)
    - Order: Entomophthorales (insect parasites)

#### **PHYLUM: GLOMEROMYCOTA** (Arbuscular Mycorrhizal Fungi)

- **Class: Glomeromycetes**
    - Order: Glomerales (plant root symbionts)

#### **PHYLUM: BLASTOCLADIOMYCOTA**

- **Class: Blastocladiomycetes**
    - Order: Blastocladiales (aquatic fungi)

#### **PHYLUM: NEOCALLIMASTIGOMYCOTA**

- **Class: Neocallimastigomycetes**
    - Order: Neocallimastigales (anaerobic gut fungi)






 **Cordyceps** are parasitic ascomycetes that specialize in controlling insect behavior, turning their hosts into zombies before erupting from their bodies

**Armillaria ostoyae** is a basidiomycete honey mushroom that forms the largest living organism on Earth, covering 2,400 acres through underground fungal networks

**Ophiocordyceps unilateralis** is an ascomycete parasite that hijacks ant brains with surgical precision, forcing them to climb to optimal spore-dispersal locations before death

**Laetiporus sulphureus** is a basidiomycete polypore known as "Chicken of the Woods" that grows in bright orange and yellow brackets resembling coral formations

**Amanita muscaria** is a basidiomycete agaric famous for its iconic red cap with white spots and potent psychoactive compounds

**Clathrus archeri** is a basidiomycete stinkhorn called "Devil's Fingers" that dramatically erupts from an egg-like structure into grotesque red tentacles

**Hydnellum peckii** is a basidiomycete tooth fungus known as "Bleeding Tooth" that oozes bright red droplets from its white surface

**Panellus stipticus** is a basidiomycete agaric called "Bitter Oyster" that glows with green bioluminescence in the dark

**Trametes versicolor** is a basidiomycete polypore known as "Turkey Tail" that displays concentric zones of multiple colors on woody substrates

**Psilocybe** are basidiomycete agarics commonly called "Magic Mushrooms" that produce powerful psychoactive compounds affecting human consciousness
