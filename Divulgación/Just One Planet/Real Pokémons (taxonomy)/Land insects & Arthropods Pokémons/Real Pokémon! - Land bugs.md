

**Major insect orders:**

- **Coleoptera** (beetles, ladybugs, rhinoceros beetles, stag beetles, dung beetles, fireflies)
- **Lepidoptera** (butterflies, moths, silk worms)
- **Hymenoptera** (bees, wasps, ants, antlions)
- **Diptera** (flies, mosquitoes, house flies, fruit flies, horseflies)
- **Orthoptera** (grasshoppers, crickets, katydids, cicadas)
- **Blattodea** (cockroaches, termites)
- **Hemiptera** (true bugs)
- **Odonata** (dragonflies, damselflies)
- **Mantodea** (mantises, praying mantis)

**Minor insect orders:**

- **Phthiraptera** (lice)
- **Thysanoptera** (thrips)
- **Phasmatodea** (stick insects)
- **Siphonaptera** (fleas)
- **Psocoptera** (booklice)
- **Zygentoma** (silverfish)
- **Ephemeroptera** (mayflies)
- **Plecoptera** (stoneflies)
- **Trichoptera** (caddisflies)
- **Megaloptera** (dobsonflies, alderflies)
- **Neuroptera** (lacewings, antlions)
- **Embioptera** (webspinners)
- **Dermaptera** (earwigs)
- **Raphidioptera** (snakeflies)

**Other terrestrial arthropods:**

- **Araneae** (spiders, tarantulas)
- **Scolopendromorpha** (giant centipedes)
- **Decapoda** (crayfish, lobsters)

**Terrestrial mollusks:**

- **Gastropoda** (slugs, snails)
