## **Cnidaria**

**Jellyfish** (_Scyphozoa_) are **Cnidaria** that are gelatinous marine animals with stinging tentacles and bell-shaped bodies that pulse for swimming

**Portuguese man o' war** (_Physalia physalis_) are **Hydrozoa** that are colonial organisms with venomous tentacles and distinctive blue gas-filled bladders for floating

**Corals** (_Anthozoa_) are **Cnidaria** that are colonial polyps building calcium carbonate reefs, vulnerable to bleaching when stressed or dying

## **Mollusca**

**Nudibranchs** (_Nudibranchia_) are **Gastropoda** that are colorful sea slugs with external gills and incredible diversity of shapes and patterns

**Octopuses** (_Octopoda_) are **Cephalopoda** that are intelligent eight-armed mollusks capable of camouflage and problem-solving

**Squids** (_Teuthida_) are **Cephalopoda** that are fast-swimming mollusks with ten arms and jet propulsion capabilities

**Seahorses** (_Hippocampus_) are actually fish, not **Chelicerata** - they are unique marine fish with horse-like heads and prehensile tails

## **Arthropoda**

**Goose barnacles** (_Pollicipes pollicipes_) are **Crustacea** that are filter-feeding arthropods with leathery stalks attached to rocky surfaces

**Giant shrimp/prawns** (_Penaeidae_) are **Crustacea** that are large decapod crustaceans with elongated bodies and swimming appendages

**Pistol shrimp** (_Alpheidae_) are **Crustacea** that are small crustaceans capable of creating cavitation bubbles with their snapping claws

**Giant marine isopods** (_Bathynomus giganteus_) are **Crustacea** that are deep-sea pill bugs growing to enormous sizes in abyssal environments

**Hermit crabs** (_Paguridae_) are **Crustacea** that are soft-bodied crabs living in borrowed shells for protection

**Crabs** (_Brachyura_) are **Crustacea** that are decapods with broad flattened bodies and sideways walking gaits

**Fiddler crabs** (_Uca_) are **Crustacea** that are small crabs with males having one greatly enlarged claw resembling a violin




**Horseshoe crabs** (_Limulidae_) are **Chelicerata** that are ancient arthropods related to spiders, not true crabs, with blue blood and horseshoe-shaped shells

## **Echinodermata**

**Brittle stars** (_Ophiuroidea_) are **Echinodermata** that are five-armed echinoderms with flexible serpentine arms for rapid movement

**Sea urchins** (_Echinoidea_) are **Echinodermata** that are spiny globular animals with calcium carbonate shells and tube feet

**Starfish** (_Asteroidea_) are **Echinodermata** that are five-armed marine animals capable of regenerating lost limbs

## **Parasitic and Specialized Forms**

**Sacculina** (_Sacculina carcini_) are **Crustacea** that are parasitic barnacles completely taking over crab hosts and controlling their behavior
