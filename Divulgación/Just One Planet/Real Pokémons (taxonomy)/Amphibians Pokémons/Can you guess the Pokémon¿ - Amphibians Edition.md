In taxonomy, amphibians are a class. It has three orders: 

- **Anura** (frogs and toads): Tailless amphibians with powerful jumping legs and vocal sacs
- **Caudata** or **Urodela** (salamanders and newts): Tailed amphibians famous for their regenerative abilities
- **Gymnophiona** (caecilians): Mysterious worm-like, legless amphibians that live underground
