**The 'mother' of the Zero Waste Movement**

Bea Johnson is a French activist known worldwide as a pioneer of the "Zero Waste" movement by her blog [Zero Waste Home](https://zerowastehome.com/).

<iframe title="vimeo-player" src="https://player.vimeo.com/video/165095064?h=5cf7153960" width="640" height="360" frameborder="0"    allowfullscreen></iframe>

Her approach is based on five fundamental principles known as the "5 R's":

1. **Refuse**
2. **Reduce**
3. **Reuse**
4. **Recycle**
5. **Rot** (Compost)

The waste she produced in a jar could be stored in a glass jar.



She wrote the book "Zero Waste Home: The Ultimate Guide to Simplifying Your Life by Reducing Your Waste" (2013), where she documents how her family of four managed to reduce their annual waste to less than a liter, contained in a small glass jar. It has been translated in 28 languages

She published the book "[Zero Waste Home: The Ultimate Guide to Simplifying Your Life by Reducing Your Waste](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/15802945-zero-waste-home)" in 2013, which has been translated to 28 languages.

Her philosophy includes a minimalist lifestyle.

Bea has given lectures around the world, has been a consultant for companies and governments, and maintains a blog where she shares practical tips for adopting this lifestyle.

Her work has inspired thousands of people to rethink their consumption habits and has significantly contributed to popularizing the concept of zero waste living globally.




El sitio oficial menciona que su trabajo ha inspirado no solo a particulares, sino también a la apertura de tiendas sin empaques, al diseño de productos reutilizables, al lanzamiento de organizaciones, e incluso a grandes corporaciones que ahora ofrecen alternativas al plástico de un solo uso.
