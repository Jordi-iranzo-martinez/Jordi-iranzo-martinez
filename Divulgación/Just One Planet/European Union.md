# Natural Environment

## Natura 2000 Network

Natura 2000 Network is the biggest and más denso system to conservate biodiversity of the world. 




The Natura 2000 protected areas network is a system of the EU to conservate its biodiversity. It comprises habitats and species. You can access the map here: [Natura 2000 viewer](https://natura2000.eea.europa.eu/).

  

The areas of the Natura 2000 network refers to the so-called Natura Directives. They are two: The "Birds Directive" and the "Habitat Directive".

- Council Directive 92/43/EEC of 21 May 1992 on the conservation of natural habitats and of wild fauna and flora
- _Directive 2009/147/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council of 30 November 2009 on the conservation of wild birds_


The Birds Directive

The Birds Directive aims for many species of birds within the Union, listed in the annexes. There is a concern also for its nests, eggs, and habitats.

Each Country has to maintain a population of those birds in an appropriate level. For that, they must have enough biodiversity and habitats for them. To achieve that, they have to create protected areas, manage them well, and create biotopes.

- **Ecosystem Restoration**: at least 25,000 km of EU rivers
- **Pesticide Reduction**: use and risk by 50%

## EU Agencies & Official Bodies

- **[European Environment Agency (EEA)](https://www.eea.europa.eu/en)** - EU agency providing independent information on the environment, including data on [European protected sites](https://www.eea.europa.eu/data-and-maps/explore-interactive-maps/european-protected-areas-1) and environmental assessments
- **[ECHA - European Chemicals Agency](https://echa.europa.eu/)** - EU agency managing chemical safety regulations, including REACH and CLP regulations for chemical substances
- **[IMPEL](https://impel.eu/)** - European Union Network for the Implementation and Enforcement of Environmental Law, supporting practical application of EU environmental legislation
- **[European Environmental Bureau (EEB)](https://eeb.org/)** - Europe's largest network of environmental citizens' organizations, advocating for sustainable policies at EU level

## Data Systems & Information Networks

- **[European Environment Information and Observation Network (EIONET)](https://www.eionet.europa.eu/)** - Partnership network providing environmental information across Europe through national focal points
- **[Water Information System for Europe (WISE)](https://water.europa.eu/)** - EU's main information platform on water policies, status, and management across European water bodies
- **[European Marine Observation and Data Network (EMODnet)](https://emodnet.ec.europa.eu/en)** - Network assembling European marine data to make it freely available for research, policy, and business
- **[SeaDataNet](https://www.seadatanet.org/)** - Pan-European infrastructure for marine and ocean data management connecting oceanographic data centers
- **[EUR-Lex](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/)** - Official portal providing free access to European Union law, including environmental legislation and regulations
